Bookmarks multiply. Tabs pile up. You remember that one doc, dashboard, or runbook — but not the URL. SlugBase is a bookmark manager built for people who live on the keyboard: save a link once, give it a **slug** in your **workspace**, and jump back with a single keystroke.

## The problem we kept hitting

Most bookmark tools are fine for saving links and forgetting them. Finding the right one later means scrolling, searching vague titles, or opening five tabs before you land on the right page. URL shorteners help with sharing, but they are not where you organize work.

SlugBase combines both: a full bookmark manager **and** private slug forwarding inside each workspace.

## How SlugBase works

Every bookmark lives in a **workspace** — your personal space or a shared team workspace. Organize with **folders** and tags, use **pinning** to keep important links at the top of the palette and dashboard, and assign a short **slug** to any bookmark you reach for often.

Open the command palette with `⌘K` (or `Ctrl+K`), type `go ` followed by a slug, and you are there. Configure a browser search shortcut on your forwarding domain for the same flow from the address bar.

<Callout type="info">
  Slugs are private to a workspace. There is no anonymous public resolution in v1 — forwarding always respects membership and sharing scope.
</Callout>

### A quick example

Save the internal docs bookmark, assign the slug `docs`, then jump to it from anywhere:

```
go docs
```

Or type your forwarding keyword plus the slug in the browser search bar — one keystroke, no hunting through folders.

## Keyboard-first by design

SlugBase is built around the command palette: search bookmarks, switch workspaces, create links, and resolve slugs without leaving the keyboard. When two bookmarks share a slug, disambiguation asks which one you mean — and you can remember your choice for next time.

Pinning keeps your most-used links visible first. Folders group related bookmarks with colored indicators; share specific folders with teammates when your workspace plan allows collaboration.

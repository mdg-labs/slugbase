# SlugBase Trademark & Brand Policy

The SlugBase **source code** is open source under the [GNU Affero General Public License v3.0](./LICENSE) (AGPL-3.0). The SlugBase **name, logo, and brand** are **not** part of that license. This policy explains how you may (and may not) use them.

The goal is simple: keep the software free and forkable, while making sure users can always tell what is the **official** SlugBase product and what is not.

## What this covers

The word marks **"SlugBase"** and **"SlugBase Cloud"**, the SlugBase logo, and other distinctive brand elements (collectively, the **"Marks"**) are trademarks of MDG Labs.

## What you may do without permission

- Use, run, study, modify, fork, and redistribute the **code** under the terms of the AGPL-3.0.
- **Self-host** SlugBase Community Edition (CE) for yourself, your company, or your clients.
- Refer to SlugBase **truthfully** — for example, "built with SlugBase", "compatible with SlugBase", "a SlugBase fork", or "based on SlugBase". This is *nominative use* and is always allowed.
- Redistribute an **unmodified** copy of the official SlugBase CE distribution under the SlugBase name, in compliance with the AGPL-3.0.

## What you may not do without permission

- Use the Marks as (or in) the **name of your own product, service, company, or domain** — especially a hosted or commercial offering — in a way that suggests it **is** SlugBase or is official or endorsed by MDG Labs.
- Use **"SlugBase Cloud"** or any confusingly similar name for a hosted service that is not operated by MDG Labs.
- **Rebrand a fork** and distribute or sell it as "SlugBase" (or a confusingly similar name).
- Use the Marks in a way that is misleading, disparaging, or that implies partnership, sponsorship, or endorsement that does not exist.
- Modify the logo or use it as your own brand identity.

## Forking guidelines

If you distribute a **modified** version, please give it **your own name** and make clear it is not the official SlugBase distribution. You may state that it is "based on SlugBase" or "a fork of SlugBase".

Retain all copyright, license, and trademark notices required by the AGPL-3.0 in source files and documentation.

## Why this exists

AGPL keeps the code open — anyone can self-host or fork it. Trademarks protect **users**: they ensure that when someone downloads "SlugBase" or signs up for "SlugBase Cloud", they are getting the real thing. This is standard practice for open-source projects and is fully compatible with the software being free and open.

## Questions

For uses not covered above — integration badges, meetups, commercial offerings that lean on the brand — contact MDG Labs before proceeding.

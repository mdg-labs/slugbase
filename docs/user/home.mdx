**Home** is the page you land on after you sign in. It shows what you use most and gets you to the command palette in one click.

<Screenshot alt="The Home page with the counts, Quick access, Pinned and Most used tags" />

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in and a workspace is open.

## Search from Home

Choose the large search field, **Search bookmarks, folders and tags**, or press `⌘K` on macOS or `Ctrl K` elsewhere. The [command palette](command-palette.mdx) opens. It searches your bookmarks, folders and tags, and it also takes `go` queries.

## Read the screen

| Part | What it shows |
| ---- | ------------- |
| The counts | How many **Bookmarks**, **Folders** and **Tags** you have. **Shared with you** also appears where your workspace can share bookmarks. |
| **Quick access** | Your most used bookmarks that have a slug and forwarding on, up to eight. Choose one to open it through its slug. Hover over it to see the address it forwards to. |
| **Pinned** | Your pinned bookmarks. Choose **View all →** to open **Bookmarks** with only the pinned ones. |
| **Most used tags** | The tags you put on most bookmarks, each with its count. Choose a tag to open **Bookmarks** with that tag. |
| **Sharing** | **Shared with you** and **Shared by you**, each a link to the matching list. It appears only where your workspace can share bookmarks. |
| **Getting started** | A checklist for your first steps. See below. |

Until you save a bookmark, Home shows **Save your first bookmark** in place of the three lists. Choose **New bookmark** to add one, or **Set up your browser** to open [Forwarding](forwarding.mdx).

Empty rows teach you what fills them. **Quick access** says: "The bookmarks with a slug that you open most appear here." **Pinned** says: "Pin a bookmark to keep it here."

<Callout>
SlugBase Cloud can show notices about your plan's limits above the totals. A self-hosted SlugBase shows none.
</Callout>

## Work through the Getting started checklist

The checklist has five items:

1. **Add a bookmark**. It ticks when you have a bookmark. Choose **New bookmark** to add one.
2. **Give a bookmark a slug**. It ticks when a bookmark has a slug. Choose **Forwarding** to learn how. See [Forwarding](forwarding.mdx).
3. **Set up the browser search engine**. Choose **Forwarding setup**, follow the steps, then choose **Mark as done** on the **Forwarding** page. SlugBase cannot see your browser, so you tick this one yourself.
4. **Create a folder**. It ticks when you have a folder. Choose **New folder**.
5. **Import from your browser**. It ticks when an import finishes. Choose **Import**.

The card shows **Getting started progress**, for example "2 of 5 done". When every item is done, the card becomes one line: **All set**.

### Hide the checklist

1. Choose **Dismiss** on the card.

### Bring the checklist back

1. Open the account menu, then **Preferences**.
2. Under **Getting started**, turn on **Show the Getting started checklist**.

## Troubleshooting

### The page says "The home screen could not be loaded"

Choose **Try again**. If it continues, check your connection or tell your administrator.

### The page says "The home screen could not be refreshed, so it may be out of date."

SlugBase shows the last data it has. Choose **Try again** to read it again.

### Quick access is empty

Only bookmarks that have a slug and forwarding on can appear. Give a bookmark a slug and turn on **Forwarding**. See [Add and Edit Bookmarks](add-and-edit-bookmarks.mdx).

### The checklist is gone

You dismissed it, or you finished every item. Turn on **Show the Getting started checklist** in **Preferences**.

### The checklist could not be dismissed

SlugBase says: "The checklist could not be dismissed. Try again in a moment." Choose **Dismiss** again.

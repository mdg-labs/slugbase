The command palette is the fastest way around SlugBase. You open it from anywhere, then search for a bookmark, run a command or open a slug.

<Screenshot alt="The command palette with a search and grouped results" />

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in and a workspace is open.

## Open the palette

- Press `⌘K` on macOS or `Ctrl K` elsewhere. It works on every page, even in a text field. Press it again to close the palette.
- Press `/` on **Bookmarks**, **Folders** or **Tags**, when no text field has focus.
- Choose **Search or run a command…** in the top bar, or the large search field on **Home**.

Choose **Close the command palette**, or press Esc, to close it.

## Start with the default view

With an empty input, the palette shows groups:

| Group | What it holds |
| ----- | ------------- |
| **Recent** | Bookmarks you opened lately. |
| **Pinned** | Your pinned bookmarks. |
| **Navigation** | **Home**, **Bookmarks**, **Folders**, **Tags**, **Forwarding** and **Settings**. |
| **Actions** | **New bookmark**, **New folder**, **Import bookmarks**, **Export bookmarks**, **Switch workspace**, **Toggle theme** and **Sign out**. |

If you have not opened or pinned anything yet, the palette says "No bookmarks to show yet" and "Bookmarks you open and bookmarks you pin appear here."

## Search

1. Type what you look for. The palette searches as you type.
2. Use the arrow keys to move through the results. They are grouped: **Bookmarks**, **Folders**, **Tags** and **Commands**.
3. Press Enter to choose.

Bookmarks are matched on their title, host, slug and description. Each result shows the slug and the host. The search tolerates small typos. Archived bookmarks never appear.

The footer shows the keys: **navigate**, **open**, **new tab**, **edit** and **close**. These keys also work:

| Keys | What they do |
| ---- | ------------ |
| Enter | Opens the bookmark, goes to the page or runs the command. |
| `⌘ Enter` or `Ctrl Enter` | Opens the bookmark in a new tab. |
| `⌥ Enter` or `Alt Enter` | Opens the bookmark dialog to edit one of your own bookmarks. |

### When nothing matches

The palette says **No results** and offers to create a bookmark: **Create a bookmark for** an address you typed, **Create a bookmark with the slug** and your text, or **Create a bookmark**. Choose it to open the bookmark dialog with that filled in. See [Add and Edit Bookmarks](add-and-edit-bookmarks.mdx).

## Use go mode

Go mode opens a slug, the way `go mail` does in your browser.

1. Type `go`, a space and the slug, such as `go mail`. You can also paste a link to `go/` and a slug on your own SlugBase.
2. A **Go** chip appears next to the input. The palette lists **Slugs** that start with your text, yours first, then those shared with you.
3. Press Enter on **Go to /go/mail**, or on a slug in the list.

**Result:** The bookmark opens, as it would from the address bar.

A link to another site is searched as text, not opened in go mode. If you type a path or a query after the slug, SlugBase does not pass it on. It looks for exactly what you typed, finds nothing, and does not offer to create a bookmark.

### Choose between several bookmarks

If several bookmarks shared with you have the slug, the palette lists them under "go/mail matches 2 bookmarks", with its host and how it is shared.

1. Optional: select **Always use this for** and the slug.
2. Choose the bookmark you mean.

With **Always use this**, SlugBase remembers the choice. Review it under **Remembered choices** on [Forwarding](forwarding.mdx).

In go mode, `⌘ Enter` or `Ctrl Enter` acts like Enter, and `⌥ Enter` or `Alt Enter` does nothing, because a slug is not a bookmark to edit.

### When the slug does not exist

The palette says "No bookmark has the slug mail in" and the workspace's name. It offers **Create a bookmark with this slug**, and, where the slug exists in another of your workspaces, **Found in:** and that workspace's name. Choose the workspace to switch and carry on.

## Field and option reference

| Field / option | Description | Required |
| -------------- | ----------- | -------- |
| Search or run a command | The input. A text searches. `go` and a slug opens a slug. | No |
| Go chip | Shows that the palette is in go mode. | n/a |
| Always use this for | Remembers your choice when a slug matches several bookmarks. | No |

## Troubleshooting

### The palette shows "The bookmarks could not be loaded."

Choose **Try again**.

### The palette shows "The search failed."

Choose **Try again**, or check your connection.

### Pressing `/` does nothing

`/` works only on **Bookmarks**, **Folders** and **Tags**, outside a text field, and only while single-key shortcuts are on. Use `⌘K` or `Ctrl K` anywhere. See [Keyboard Shortcuts](keyboard-shortcuts.mdx).

### My bookmark does not appear

It may be archived, or it is in another workspace. Choose **Switch workspace** in the palette.

### Go mode says the slug could not be loaded

SlugBase says "The slugs could not be loaded." Choose **Try again**.

**Forwarding** is the home of slugs. Here you add SlugBase to your browser as a search engine, turn forwarding on or off for each slug, and review the choices go remembered for you.

<Screenshot alt="The Forwarding page with Set up your browser, Your slugs and Remembered choices" />

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in and a workspace is open.
- You use Chrome, Firefox, Edge or Safari on a desktop.

## Set up your browser

Once your browser knows SlugBase as a search engine, typing `go mail` in the address bar opens the bookmark with the slug `mail`.

1. Under **Set up your browser**, choose **Copy the search engine address**. The address has the form `https://<your SlugBase host>/go/%s`. The host is the one your SlugBase is set up with. The `%s` stands for what you type after the keyword.
2. Note the **Keyword**, which is `go`. Choose **Copy the keyword** to copy it.

Then add the address as a search engine in your browser. Under **Browser**, choose your browser and follow its steps. They are shown here too. Paste the address you copied where the steps ask for the URL.

<Tabs>
<Tab label="Chrome">

1. Open Settings, then Search engine, then Manage search engines and site search.
2. Under Site search, choose Add.
3. Enter SlugBase as the name and `go` as the shortcut, and paste the address as the URL.
4. Choose Add to save it.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Firefox">

1. Open Settings and choose Search.
2. Under Search Shortcuts, choose Add.
3. Enter SlugBase as the name, paste the address as the URL and enter `go` as the alias.
4. Save the search engine.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Edge">

1. Open Settings, then Privacy, search, and services, then Address bar and search.
2. Choose Manage search engines, then Add.
3. Enter SlugBase as the search engine and `go` as the shortcut, and paste the address as the URL.
4. Choose Add to save it.

</Tab>
<Tab label="Safari">

1. Safari has no setting for a custom search engine.
2. Install a Safari extension that adds custom search engines.
3. In the extension, add SlugBase with the address you copied and the keyword `go`.

</Tab>
</Tabs>

## Test it and mark it done

1. Choose **Test it: open go/** followed by a slug of yours. It opens that address in a new tab. Once the search engine is set up, type `go` and the slug in the address bar.
2. Choose **Mark as done**. The page shows **Marked as done**, and the **Getting started** checklist on Home ticks **Set up the browser search engine**.

If none of your bookmarks has a slug that forwards yet, the test link opens a sample address. SlugBase then answers that no bookmark has that slug, and offers to create one.

## Manage your slugs

**Your slugs** lists the bookmarks of yours that have a slug. **Forwarding** decides whether the slug takes you to the bookmark when you go to it.

1. Type in **Search your slugs** to filter by slug, title or host.
2. Use the switch in the **Forwarding** column to turn forwarding on or off for one slug. The switch is named **Forwarding for** and the slug.
3. Open the actions menu, **Actions for** and the slug, for **Edit**, **Copy address** or **Turn off forwarding**.

The list shows the **Slug**, the **Destination** host, **Forwarding**, **Opens** and **Last opened**. A slug that was never used shows **Never**.

When a bookmark has forwarding off, it keeps its slug but go does not open it. The slug still helps in search and the palette.

## Review your remembered choices

When several bookmarks shared with you have the same slug, go asks which one you mean. If you chose **Always use this**, SlugBase remembers it. **Remembered choices** lists them: "When several bookmarks share a slug, go sends you to the one you chose to always use."

1. Find the slug under **Slug**. The list shows the **Bookmark**, its **Destination** and its **Owner**, where **You** marks your own.
2. Choose **Remove**.

**Result:** SlugBase says "Forgot your choice for" and the slug. Choose **Undo** to bring it back.

SlugBase also forgets a choice by itself when its bookmark is deleted, when the bookmark's slug changes or its forwarding goes off, and when you lose access to the bookmark.

## Field and option reference

| Field / option | Description | Required |
| -------------- | ----------- | -------- |
| Search engine address | The address to paste into your browser. It ends in `/go/%s`. | n/a |
| Keyword | `go`. What you type before the slug. | n/a |
| Forwarding | A switch per slug. Off keeps the slug, and go does not open the bookmark. | No |
| Remove | Forgets a remembered choice. | No |

## Troubleshooting

### The page shows "No slugs yet"

None of your bookmarks has a slug. Choose **New bookmark** and give one a slug. See [Add and Edit Bookmarks](add-and-edit-bookmarks.mdx).

### Typing `go mail` searches the web instead

The browser did not take the search engine. Check the shortcut or keyword, and that the address contains `%s`. In Safari, use an extension.

### A slug opens "No bookmark has the slug"

The slug does not exist in the open workspace, or its forwarding is off. Check the switch under **Forwarding**. See [Go Pages](go.mdx).

### The switch jumps back

SlugBase says "Forwarding could not be turned on. It is off again." or "Forwarding could not be turned off. It is on again." Try again in a moment. "This bookmark was changed elsewhere, so forwarding was not changed. The list is up to date again." means someone changed the bookmark first.

### The setup could not be marked as done

SlugBase says: "The setup could not be marked as done. Try again in a moment." Choose **Mark as done** again.

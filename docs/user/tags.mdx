**Tags** lists your tags with a count for each, and previews the newest bookmarks of the tag you pick.

<Screenshot alt="The Tags page with the tag list and the preview of a tag" />

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in and a workspace is open.

## How tags work

- A tag labels bookmarks across folders, such as a topic or a project. A bookmark can carry several.
- Tags are private to you. The page says: "Tags are private to you. Other members of the workspace never see them."
- You add a tag when you save or edit a bookmark. See [Add and Edit Bookmarks](add-and-edit-bookmarks.mdx). A tag name has 1 to 40 characters.

## Browse your tags

1. Open **Tags**.
2. Type in **Search tags** to find a tag by its name. Choose **Clear search** to see all.
3. Under **Sort by**, choose **Name** or **Count**.
4. Select a tag. The preview shows **Newest bookmarks tagged** and the tag's name, with each bookmark's **Bookmark** title and **Added** date.
5. Choose **View all in Bookmarks** to open **Bookmarks** with every bookmark that carries the tag.

On a narrow screen, the preview opens in a side panel. Choose **Close the preview** to close it.

Each tag shows how many bookmarks carry it, with a bar that shows its size next to the others. The address of the page keeps the tag you picked, the sort and the search, so you can link to the view.

## Rename a tag

1. Open the actions menu, **Actions for** and the tag's name, and choose **Rename**.
2. Enter the new name under **Name**.
3. Choose **Save**.

**Result:** SlugBase says **Tag renamed.**

If you already have a tag with the new name, SlugBase offers to merge. The dialog **Merge into** and the name explains what happens: the bookmarks with the old tag get the other tag, and the old tag is deleted. The bookmarks stay. Choose **Merge tags** to confirm, or **Back to rename**.

## Delete a tag

1. Open the actions menu of the tag and choose **Delete**.
2. Read the confirmation. For a tag on 9 bookmarks it says: "The tag is taken off its 9 bookmarks. The bookmarks stay; only the tag goes."
3. Choose **Delete tag**.

**Result:** SlugBase says **Tag deleted.**

## Field and option reference

| Field / option | Description | Required |
| -------------- | ----------- | -------- |
| Search tags | Finds tags whose name contains your text. | No |
| Sort by | **Name** or **Count**. | No |
| Name | The tag's name, 1 to 40 characters, unique among your tags. | Yes, when renaming |

## Troubleshooting

### The page shows "No tags yet"

You have no tags. Add one when you save or edit a bookmark, or choose **Go to Bookmarks**.

### The page shows "No tag matches"

None of your tags contains your search text. Choose **Clear search**.

### The form says "You already have a tag with this name."

You are renaming a tag to a name another tag has. SlugBase offers to merge them instead.

### The preview says "This tag does not exist."

The tag may have been renamed, merged or deleted. Pick another tag.

### The tags could not be loaded

SlugBase says "The tags could not be loaded. Try again in a moment." Choose **Try again**.

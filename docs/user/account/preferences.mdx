**Preferences** sets how SlugBase looks and how it reacts to your keyboard. Each choice saves as soon as you make it.

<Screenshot alt="The Preferences page with Appearance, Keyboard and Getting started" />

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in.

## Open your preferences

1. Open the account menu in the top bar and choose **Account settings**.
2. Under **Account**, choose **Preferences**.

## Change how SlugBase looks

Under **Appearance**:

1. Choose a **Theme**: **System**, **Dark** or **Light**. **System** follows the setting of your device.
2. Choose an **Accent colour**: **Periwinkle**, **Violet**, **Rose**, **Amber**, **Green** or **Teal**. It colours buttons, switches and the focus ring.
3. Choose a **Default bookmark view**: **Grid** or **Table**. It decides how your bookmark lists open. See [Bookmarks](../bookmarks.mdx).

**Result:** the page says **Saving…** and then **All changes saved**. The theme and the accent apply at once. The top bar also has a button that switches between the light and the dark theme.

## Turn single-key shortcuts on or off

Under **Keyboard**, **Single-key shortcuts** lets you act with one key, for example `C` to create a bookmark. Turn it off if keys are triggered by accident. "Shortcuts that hold Ctrl or Command always work." See [Keyboard Shortcuts](../keyboard-shortcuts.mdx).

## Bring back the Getting started checklist

Under **Getting started**, turn on **Show the Getting started checklist** to bring the checklist on [Home](../home.mdx) back after you dismissed it. Turn it off to hide it.

## Stop AI suggestions for yourself

This section appears only when your SlugBase has an AI provider. Under **AI suggestions**, turn on **Don't use AI suggestions for me**. "No bookmark of yours is sent to the AI service." A workspace admin can also switch suggestions off for the whole workspace. See [Add and Edit Bookmarks](../add-and-edit-bookmarks.mdx).

## Field and option reference

| Field / option | Description | Required |
| -------------- | ----------- | -------- |
| Theme | **System**, **Dark** or **Light**. | No |
| Accent colour | One of six colours. | No |
| Default bookmark view | **Grid** or **Table**. | No |
| Single-key shortcuts | Act with one key. On by default. | No |
| Show the Getting started checklist | Shows or hides the checklist on Home. | No |
| Don't use AI suggestions for me | Only where AI is set up. | No |

The language is not here. You choose it on [Profile](profile.mdx).

## Troubleshooting

### The page says "This change was not saved."

SlugBase could not save the last change. Make the change again. If it keeps failing, reload the page.

### The page says "Your preferences could not be loaded"

Choose **Try again**. If it continues, tell the person who runs your SlugBase.

### There is no AI section

Your SlugBase has no AI provider set up, so no bookmark of yours can be sent to one.

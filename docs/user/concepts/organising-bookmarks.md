SlugBase gives you three ways to organise bookmarks: folders, tags and pinning. You use all three on your own bookmarks. Only folders can be shared with other members.

## Folders

A folder is a named container for related bookmarks, such as everything for one project. These rules apply:

- A folder name has 1 to 64 characters. You cannot have two folders with the same name, in any capitalisation.
- You can give a folder an icon and one of eight colours.
- Folders are flat. A folder cannot hold another folder.
- A bookmark can be in several folders, and a folder can hold any number of bookmarks.
- There is no limit on how many folders you can make.
- You file only your own bookmarks, and only into your own folders.

Create a folder with **New folder** on the **Folders** page, or type a new name in the **Folders** field of the bookmark dialog and choose **Create folder**. For each folder you can **Rename**, **Change icon and colour**, **Share settings** and **Delete**. Deleting a folder never deletes its bookmarks. They stay, and only the folder goes.

Your folders also appear in the sidebar. Choose one to see only its bookmarks.

### Shared folders

You can share a folder with a member or with a team. Everyone you share it with can read the folder and every bookmark in it, now and later. Their slugs work for them too. They cannot change the folder or its bookmarks. Only you can.

A folder shared with you shows **Shared with you** or **Shared with you by** and the owner's name. On the **Folders** page, choose **Shared with me** to list them.

## Tags

A tag is a label such as a topic or a project. A bookmark can carry several tags, and a tag works across folders.

Tags are private to you. Nobody else in the workspace sees them, not even on a bookmark you share. People who read a bookmark you shared see it without your tags.

- A tag name has 1 to 40 characters. You cannot have two tags with the same name, in any capitalisation.
- Add a tag when you save or edit a bookmark. Type a name in the **Tags** field and choose **Create tag**.
- On the **Tags** page you see each tag with the number of bookmarks that carry it. Select a tag to preview its newest bookmarks, and choose **View all in Bookmarks** to see them all.
- **Rename** a tag to change its name. If you already have a tag with the new name, SlugBase offers to merge the two. The bookmarks of the old tag get the other tag, and the old tag is deleted.
- **Delete** a tag to take it off its bookmarks. The bookmarks stay.

## Pinning

Pinning marks a bookmark as one you want within reach. It is the only way to mark a bookmark as prominent.

- Pin a bookmark with **Pin** on its row, with the **Pinned** switch in the bookmark dialog, or with **Pin** in the bar that appears when you select several bookmarks. Press `P` to pin or unpin the focused bookmark.
- Pinned bookmarks come first on **Home**, under **Pinned**, up to 12 of them. **View all** lists them in full.
- On the **Bookmarks** page, the **Pinned** filter shows only pinned bookmarks. The command palette lists them in a **Pinned** group.
- Pinning is yours. You can pin only your own bookmarks, and other members never see your pins. A bookmark shared with you can be read but not pinned.

## Choosing between them

| | Folders | Tags | Pinning |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Use it for | Grouping bookmarks that belong together | Labelling bookmarks across groups | Keeping a few bookmarks at hand |
| On one bookmark | In several folders | Several tags | Pinned or not |
| Who can see it | You, and anyone you share the folder with | Only you | Only you |

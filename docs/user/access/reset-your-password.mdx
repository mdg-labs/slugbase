**Forgot your password?** sends you a link, and **Choose a new password** saves a new password and signs you in.

<Screenshot alt="The Forgot your password page with the email field" />

## Prerequisites

- Your account has a password and a verified email address. A reset link goes only to such accounts.
- The server can send email. See the section on a server without mail below.

## Ask for a reset link

1. On **Sign in**, choose **Forgot password?**.
2. Enter your **Email**.
3. Choose **Send the link**.

**Result:** The page says: "If an account exists for that address, we sent a link." The link works for 1 hour. If it does not arrive, check your spam folder.

SlugBase gives this answer for every address, so it never reveals which addresses have an account.

## Choose a new password

1. Open the link in the email. **Choose a new password** appears.
2. Enter your new **Password**. Use at least 12 characters. The meter shows **Password strength**.
3. Choose **Save password and sign in**.

**Result:** You are signed in with the new password. Saving it signs you out of every other session of this account. SlugBase also ends your API tokens and the sign-in providers you linked, because they were made before the reset. Create them again in your account settings.

If your account has two-step verification, **Two-step verification** appears next. Enter your code to finish. A reset never skips the second step.

## On a server without mail

When the server cannot send email, the page shows **Ask your instance admin to send you a reset link** and says: "This server cannot send email, so it cannot send the link itself." Ask an instance admin of your SlugBase. On SlugBase Cloud, ask support.

## Field and option reference

| Field / option | Description | Required |
| -------------- | ----------- | -------- |
| Email | The address of your account. | Yes |
| Password | Your new password: at least 12 characters, at most 256. | Yes |

## Troubleshooting

### The page says "This link is not valid or has expired"

A link works once and for 1 hour. A newer request also replaces the earlier link. Choose **Request a new link** and ask again.

### No email arrives

Check your spam folder and the address you typed. SlugBase sends the link only to an account that has a password and a verified address. If you never verified your address, use the link in your verification email, or open [Create an Account](create-an-account.mdx). If your account signs in only through an identity provider, sign in with the provider.

### A password is refused

SlugBase refuses a password with fewer than 12 characters, and one that appears in a list of breached passwords: "This password appears in a list of breached passwords. Choose a different one."

### The page says "Too many requests"

Wait for the time the page names, then try again.

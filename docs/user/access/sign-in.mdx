**Sign in** opens your workspace with your email and password, and, where your account asks for it, a second step.

<Screenshot alt="The Sign in page with email, password and Remember me" />

## Prerequisites

- You have an account. If you do not, see [Create an Account](create-an-account.mdx) or [Accept an Invitation](accept-an-invitation.mdx).

## Sign in with email and password

1. Open your SlugBase. The page **Sign in** appears when you are signed out.
2. Enter your **Email** and **Password**.
3. Select **Remember me** to stay signed in for longer on this device. Leave it off on a computer you share.
4. Choose **Sign in**.

**Result:** Home opens. If you were on the way to a page or a slug when SlugBase asked you to sign in, it takes you there instead.

If your account has two-step verification, **Two-step verification** appears after you choose **Sign in**.

## Finish two-step verification

If you turned on two-step verification in your account security settings, the password is only the first step.

1. Open your authenticator app and read the six-digit code.
2. Enter it under **Authentication code**.
3. Choose **Verify**.

If you cannot use your authenticator app, choose **Use a backup code instead**, enter one of your backup codes under **Backup code**, and choose **Verify**. Choose **Use the authenticator app instead** to go back to the code field. Each backup code works once.

Choose **Back to sign in** to start again. You have about five minutes for this step. When the time runs out, SlugBase says **Your sign-in has expired**. Sign in again to continue. Five wrong codes in a row also end the attempt.

## Sign in with an identity provider

Where the operator of your SlugBase set up an identity provider, buttons named **Continue with** and the provider's name appear under the form, after the word **or**.

1. Choose the **Continue with** button of your provider.
2. Sign in at the provider.

**Result:** You are back in SlugBase, signed in. A sign-in through a provider does not ask for the SlugBase code, because the provider already checked you.

With no provider set up, the page shows only the email and password form.

## Notices on the page

The operator of a self-hosted SlugBase can add a notice to the sign-in page. It appears above the form.

The link **Create an account** appears under the form only where registration is open. On a self-hosted SlugBase it is off unless the operator turned it on. See [Create an Account](create-an-account.mdx).

## Field and option reference

| Field / option | Description | Required |
| -------------- | ----------- | -------- |
| Email | The address of your account. | Yes |
| Password | Your password. **Show password** and **Hide password** reveal or hide it. | Yes |
| Remember me | Keeps you signed in for longer on this device: a 90-day session instead of a 30-day one. | No |
| Forgot password? | Opens [Reset Your Password](reset-your-password.mdx). | No |
| Authentication code | The six-digit code of your authenticator app. | Yes, on the second step |
| Backup code | One of your backup codes, when you cannot use the app. | Yes, instead of the code |

## Troubleshooting

### The page says "The email address or the password is wrong."

Check both entries. SlugBase gives this one message whatever the reason, so it never reveals which addresses have an account. A disabled account sees the same message. If you forgot the password, choose **Forgot password?**. If a disabled account is the cause, ask the person who runs your SlugBase.

### The page says "Verify your email"

Your address is not verified yet. Open the link in the email we sent you, or choose **Send the link again** and enter your **Email**. After a request, the button shows a countdown before you can send again. See [Create an Account](create-an-account.mdx).

### The page says "Too many sign-in attempts"

Too many attempts came from your network or for your address. Wait for the time the page names and try again.

### The page says "Signing in with that provider did not work."

The provider did not confirm you. Try again, or sign in with your email address and password. If it keeps failing, tell the person who runs your SlugBase.

### I lost my authenticator app and my backup codes

SlugBase cannot let you in without a code. On a self-hosted SlugBase, ask an instance admin to reset two-step verification for your account. On SlugBase Cloud, contact support. After the reset, you sign in with your password alone and can turn two-step verification on again.

### A message says "Your session has ended"

Your session is over, so SlugBase asks you to sign in again. Open **Sign in** and enter your details to continue.

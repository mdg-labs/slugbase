**Create an account** registers you with a name, an email address and a password, and **Verify your email** finishes it.

<Screenshot alt="The Create an account page with name, email and password" />

<Callout>
Registration is on by default on SlugBase Cloud. A self-hosted SlugBase has it off unless the operator turned it on. Without it, you join through an invitation. See [Accept an Invitation](accept-an-invitation.mdx).
</Callout>

## Prerequisites

- Registration is open on your SlugBase. If it is not, the page says **Registration is closed**.
- You can open email at the address you register with, where the server sends email.

## Create your account

1. Open **Create an account**. On the page **Sign in**, the link **Create an account** leads there.
2. Enter your **Name**, **Email** and **Password**. Use at least 12 characters. The meter under **Password** shows **Password strength**.
3. Choose **Create account**.

**Result:** The page **Check your email** names your address and says that, if SlugBase can use it, a link to verify it is on its way: "Open it to finish creating your account." The link works for 24 hours.

<Callout>
SlugBase Cloud asks you to accept its terms on this page.
</Callout>

## Verify your email

1. Open the link in the email.
2. On **Verify your email**, enter your **Password**. This is the password you will use to sign in.
3. Choose **Verify and sign in**.

**Result:** You are signed in, and your address is verified. SlugBase creates a personal workspace for you, named after you, and Home opens.

A link works once.

On a server that cannot send email, registration does not wait for a link. The page **Your account is ready** says: "This server does not send email, so there is no link to open. Sign in to continue." Choose **Go to sign in**.

## Field and option reference

| Field / option | Description | Required |
| -------------- | ----------- | -------- |
| Name | Your display name. | Yes |
| Email | The address you sign in with, and where the verification link goes. | Yes |
| Password | At least 12 characters and at most 256. A long phrase works well. | Yes |
| Send the link again | Sends a new verification link. It shows a countdown after you use it. | No |

## Troubleshooting

### The page says "Registration is closed"

The operator of this SlugBase turned off public registration. Ask an admin for an invitation, or choose **Back to sign in** if you already have an account.

### The page says "An account cannot be created with that email address."

SlugBase cannot use that address for a new account. Check it, or try another address. If it keeps failing, ask the person who runs your SlugBase.

### The page says "Registration needs email, and this server cannot send any right now. Ask an administrator."

This server requires you to verify your address and cannot send the link at the moment. Ask the person who runs your SlugBase.

### The email does not arrive

Check your spam folder, and check that you typed the address correctly. A self-hosted server needs mail set up to send it.

If you already have an account for that address, SlugBase shows the same **Check your email** page and creates nothing, so it never reveals which addresses have an account. Open [Sign In](sign-in.mdx) instead, or choose **Forgot password?** there.

To get the verification link again, enter your **Email** and **Password** on **Sign in** and choose **Sign in**. The message **Verify your email** appears with the button **Send the link again**.

### The page says "This link is not valid or has expired"

A link works once and for 24 hours. Enter your **Email** on the page to get a new one.

### The page says "Too many registrations"

Wait for the time the page names, then try again.

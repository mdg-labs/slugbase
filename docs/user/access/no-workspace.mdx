**You are not in a workspace yet** appears when your account is signed in but has no workspace open.

<Screenshot alt="The You are not in a workspace yet page with Create a workspace" />

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in.

## Create a workspace

Where your account can create one, the page says: "Create a workspace to start saving bookmarks."

1. Choose **Create a workspace**.
2. In the **New workspace** dialog, enter a **Name**. Use 1 to 64 characters.
3. Choose **Create workspace**.

**Result:** You own the new workspace, and Home opens in it.

## Wait for an invitation

When your account cannot create a workspace, the page shows **Waiting for an invitation** and says: "Ask the owner of a workspace to invite you. It appears here once you accept."

1. Ask the owner or an admin of a workspace to invite your email address.
2. Open the link in the invitation. See [Accept an Invitation](accept-an-invitation.mdx).

The page shows the address you are signed in with under **Signed in as**. Choose **Sign out** to use another account.

## Choose a workspace

If you belong to workspaces but none is open, the page shows **Choose a workspace**: "You are a member of these workspaces, but none is open." Choose **Open** followed by a workspace name.

## Field and option reference

| Field / option | Description | Required |
| -------------- | ----------- | -------- |
| Create a workspace | Opens the dialog to make a workspace you own. It appears only where your account may create one. | No |
| Name | The name of the new workspace, 1 to 64 characters. | Yes |
| Open the admin area | Opens the admin area. It appears for an instance admin only, on a self-hosted SlugBase. | No |
| Sign out | Ends your session. | No |

## Troubleshooting

### The page says "Waiting for an invitation" and I expected to create a workspace

On a self-hosted SlugBase, the instance admin decides whether members can create workspaces, and the setting is off by default. Ask to be invited, or ask the instance admin to allow it.

### The dialog says "Verify your email address to create a workspace."

Open the link in your verification email first. See [Create an Account](create-an-account.mdx).

### The dialog says "You have reached the limit of workspaces for your account."

Your account owns as many workspaces as it may. On SlugBase Cloud, the limit depends on your plan. See https://slugbase.app/pricing.

### The page says "Your account could not be loaded"

Choose **Try again**. If it continues, tell your administrator.

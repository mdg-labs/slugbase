**Teams** lists the teams of the workspace and who is in them. Admins and owners create, edit and delete teams. A team is a group of members that you can share with in one step.

<Screenshot alt="The Teams page with the list of teams and their members" />

## Prerequisites

- You are signed in and a workspace is open.
- To create, edit or delete a team, you are an admin or an owner.

<Callout>
On a self-hosted SlugBase, teams are always on. On SlugBase Cloud, your plan decides whether the workspace can use teams. See [pricing](https://slugbase.app/pricing) for the plans.
</Callout>

## What each role can do here

| What | Owner | Admin | Member |
| ---- | :---: | :---: | :----: |
| See the teams and who is in them | Yes | Yes | Yes, read-only |
| Create, edit and delete a team | Yes | Yes | No |
| Put a person in a team | Yes | Yes | No |
| Share a bookmark or folder with a team | Yes | Yes | Yes |

A member sees the page with the line "The teams of this workspace and who is in them. Admins and owners change them." and no buttons. [Teams and Invitations](../concepts/teams-and-invitations.mdx) explains what a team is and does.

## Open Teams

1. Open the account menu in the top bar and choose **Account settings**.
2. Under **Workspace**, choose **Teams**.

The list shows each team's **Name**, **Description** and **Members**, with their avatars and a count. Choose **Load more** to see further teams.

## Create a team

1. Choose **Create team**.
2. Enter a **Name**. Use 1 to 64 characters. Names are unique in the workspace.
3. Optional: enter a **Description**. Use up to 280 characters. A counter shows how many you used.
4. Under **Members**, search for members and choose them. Choose a name's remove button to take a person out again.
5. Choose **Create team**.

**Result:** SlugBase says **Team created.** and the team appears in the list. The form says: "A team is shared by the whole workspace."

## Edit a team

1. Open the actions menu of the team, **Actions for the team** and its name, and choose **Edit**.
2. Change the **Name**, the **Description** or the **Members**.
3. Choose **Save changes**.

**Result:** SlugBase says **Team saved.** Removing someone from a team ends their access to anything shared only through that team, at once. Adding someone gives them access to everything shared with the team.

You can also assign one person to several teams from [Members](members.mdx), with **Manage teams**.

## Delete a team

1. Open the actions menu of the team and choose **Delete**.
2. Read the warning: "Everything shared through the team" and its name "stops being shared with its members. The members stay in the workspace."
3. Choose **Delete team**.

**Result:** SlugBase says the team was deleted. The shares through it are gone.

## Share with a team

Anyone in the workspace can share their own bookmarks and folders with a team. See [Share Dialog](../sharing.mdx).

## Field and option reference

| Field / option | Description | Required |
| -------------- | ----------- | -------- |
| Name | 1 to 64 characters, unique in the workspace. | Yes |
| Description | Up to 280 characters. | No |
| Members | The people in the team. Search by name. | No |

## Troubleshooting

### The page says "No teams yet"

The workspace has no teams. An admin or owner can choose **Create team**. Members see: "An admin or owner can create one to group members and share access."

### The form says "The workspace already has a team with this name."

Choose another name.

### The form says "This team was changed somewhere else."

Close the form and open it again to see the latest version.

### The form says "Someone you chose is no longer a member of this workspace."

Remove that person from the list and save again.

### The form says "Some of your changes were saved, but not all of them."

The name, description and members are saved in separate steps, and one failed. Choose the save button again to send the rest.

### The page says "The teams could not be loaded"

Choose **Try again**.

**Members** lists the people in the workspace and what each of them can do. Admins and owners use it to invite people, change roles, remove members, hand over ownership, and see, reassign and delete the bookmarks and folders that former members left behind.

<Screenshot alt="The Members page with the members table and the pending invitations" />

## Prerequisites

- You are an admin or an owner of the workspace. A member who opens this page sees "You don't have access" and finds only **General** and **Teams** in the settings navigation.

<Callout>
On a self-hosted SlugBase, invitations and teams are always on. On SlugBase Cloud, your plan decides whether the workspace can invite people and use teams, and a seat summary appears above the invitations. See [pricing](https://slugbase.app/pricing) for the plans.
</Callout>

## What each role can do here

| What | Owner | Admin | Member |
| ---- | :---: | :---: | :----: |
| Open this page | Yes | Yes | No |
| Invite people, resend, copy a link or revoke an invitation | Yes | Yes | No |
| Change a role between admin and member | Yes | Yes, but not an owner's | No |
| Make someone an owner, or change an owner's role | Yes | No | No |
| Transfer ownership | Yes | No | No |
| See, reassign and delete the content of former members | Yes | Yes | No |
| Remove a member | Yes | Yes, but not an owner | No |
| Remove an owner | Yes | No | No |

A workspace always has at least one owner. The last owner cannot be demoted, removed or leave. See [Workspaces and Roles](../concepts/workspaces-and-roles.mdx).

## Open Members

1. Open the account menu in the top bar and choose **Account settings**.
2. Under **Workspace**, choose **Members**.

The table shows each member's **Name**, with **You** next to your own, **Email**, **Role**, **Teams**, **Joined** and **Last active**. **Last active** says **Never** for someone who has not used the workspace yet. Choose **Show more** to see further members.

## Invite people

1. Under **Invitations**, choose **Invite people**.
2. Enter one or more **Email addresses**. Separate them with a comma, or press Enter after each.
3. Choose a **Role**: **Admin** or **Member**. An invitation cannot make someone an owner.
4. Optional: pick **Teams**. The people you invite join them when they accept. This field appears only where your workspace can use teams.
5. Choose **Send invitations**.

**Result:** SlugBase lists the outcome for each address: **Invited**, **Skipped** or **Failed**. It says, for example, "Already a member of this workspace." for someone who is in. Choose **Try the failed ones again** to resend only the failed ones.

Inviting an address that already has a pending invitation sends it again, with the role and teams of your new request. See [Teams and Invitations](../concepts/teams-and-invitations.mdx) for what the person sees when they accept.

If your SlugBase cannot send email, the dialog says so. The invitations are created without a message. Copy each link from the list and send it yourself.

## Manage pending invitations

**Invitations** lists people who were invited and have not joined yet, with their **Email**, **Role**, who they were **Invited by**, the **Status** (**Pending** or **Expired**) and when it **Expires**. Open the actions menu of a row, **Actions for the invitation of** and the address:

| Action | What it does |
| ------ | ------------ |
| **Resend** | Sends the invitation again by email, with a new link and a new expiry. The old link stops working. It appears only where your SlugBase can send email. You can resend an expired invitation. |
| **Copy invitation link** | Shows a new link once, in **Invitation link**. Send it to the person yourself. Any earlier link for this invitation stops working, and the new one expires in 7 days. Use it when your SlugBase cannot send email. |
| **Revoke** | Asks "Revoke the invitation for" the address. The link stops working at once, and you can invite the person again later. |

A link shows only once. If you lose it, choose **Copy invitation link** again for a new one.

## See, reassign and delete the content of former members

When someone deletes their account, or leaves or is removed and their content has no new owner, their bookmarks and folders stay in the workspace without an owner. **Former members' content** lists them, below **Invitations**. Only admins and owners see this section.

**Bookmarks** shows each bookmark's **Title**, **Host**, **Slug** and when it was **Created**. A bookmark without a slug says **No slug**. **Folders** shows each folder's **Name**, how many bookmarks from this list are filed in it under **Bookmarks**, and when it was **Created**. Choose **Show more** to see further rows.

**Result:** you see what has no owner. When there is nothing, the section says "No content from former members".

The lists leave out a bookmark that a plan archived, and the content of a member who just left until SlugBase has applied the choice made when they left.

To give content that has no owner to a member:

1. Select the rows you want to give away. Use the checkbox in a row, or the checkbox in a table's header to select all the rows loaded so far.
2. Choose **Reassign to…**. The button stays off while nothing is selected.
3. In **New owner**, choose a member of the workspace. You can choose yourself. The list shows current members only.
4. Choose **Continue**.
5. Read the message. It says who becomes the owner of how many bookmarks and folders, and what the move changes.
6. Choose **Reassign**.

**Result:** SlugBase says what it moved, for example "3 bookmarks and 1 folder reassigned to Grace Hopper.", and loads both lists again. The member now owns the content and sees it in their own bookmarks and folders. The workspace's bookmark count does not change, and the shares and tags of the content stay as they are. The [Audit Log](audit-log.mdx) records that someone reassigned former members' content, with how many bookmarks and folders and who received them, but not their titles.

The move keeps what the member already has:

- A bookmark whose slug the member already uses arrives without its slug, and it no longer forwards. The message says how many slugs SlugBase removed.
- A folder whose name the member already has, in any capitalisation, arrives with a number added, for example "Reading (2)". The message says how many folders SlugBase renamed.
- A folder and a bookmark filed in it stay together only when the same member owns both. If you give away a folder without a bookmark filed in it, or a bookmark without its folder, SlugBase takes the bookmark out of the folder. The message says how many filings SlugBase removed. To keep a folder and its bookmarks together, select them all in one selection, and reassign them in one go.

One reassignment takes at most 1,000 bookmarks and 1,000 folders. When you select more, **Reassign to…** stays off and a message says so. Reassign in smaller selections, and select each folder together with its bookmarks in the same selection: a folder and a bookmark you give away in two separate reassignments are no longer filed together, because the first reassignment has taken one of them out of the other's folder.

SlugBase only moves content that has no owner. If a row you selected got an owner, or was already gone, it stays or is skipped, and the message says "Nothing was reassigned" when none of them could be moved. If the member you chose has left the workspace in the meantime, the dialog says so by name, keeps your selection and asks you to choose another member. If the move fails for another reason, SlugBase shows "The change was not saved", and your selection stays so that you can try again.

To delete content that has no owner:

1. Select the rows you want to delete. Use the checkbox in a row, or the checkbox in a table's header to select all the rows loaded so far.
2. Choose **Delete selected**. The button stays off while nothing is selected.
3. Read the message. It says how many bookmarks and folders go, for example "Deleting 3 bookmarks and 1 folder is permanent."
4. Choose **Delete**.

**Result:** SlugBase says what it deleted, for example "3 bookmarks and 1 folder deleted.", and loads both lists again. The deletion also removes the shares, tags and filings in folders of what it deletes. There is no trash and no undo. Deleting a folder does not delete the bookmarks filed in it. Select them too to delete them. The workspace's bookmark count goes down by the bookmarks deleted, and the [Audit Log](audit-log.mdx) records that someone deleted former members' content, with how many bookmarks and folders, but not their titles.

SlugBase only deletes content that has no owner. If a row you selected got an owner, or was already gone, it stays or is skipped, and the message says "Nothing was deleted" when none of them could be deleted. If the deletion fails, SlugBase shows "The change was not saved", and your selection stays so that you can try again.

## Change a role

1. In the **Role** column, choose the new role from the list in the person's row. Or open **Actions for** and the person's name, and choose **Make owner**, **Make admin** or **Make member**.

**Result:** the role changes at once. The role changes what the person can do in the workspace, for example whether they can open this page.

Making someone an owner, or changing an owner's role, asks you to confirm it is you. See the section **Confirm it is you** below.

SlugBase can refuse a change:

- "A workspace needs at least one owner. Make someone else an owner first."
- "You are not allowed to change this role." An admin cannot change an owner's role.
- "Verify your email address before you make someone an owner or give up your own owner role in a workspace you made."

## Assign teams to a member

1. Open **Actions for** and the person's name, and choose **Manage teams**.
2. In **Teams of** and the person's name, search under **Teams** and choose the teams.
3. Choose **Save teams**.

**Result:** SlugBase says the teams were saved. **Manage teams** appears only where your workspace can use teams. See [Teams](teams.mdx).

## Remove a member

1. Open **Actions for** and the person's name, and choose **Remove from workspace**. You cannot remove yourself here. Use **Leave workspace** on [General](general.mdx).
2. In **Remove** and the person's name, choose what happens to their bookmarks and folders:
   - **Transfer the content.** The bookmarks and folders go to another member and keep their slugs. A slug the recipient already uses is cleared on the transferred bookmark. Under **Transfer to**, search for the recipient. SlugBase starts with you.
   - **Delete the content.** The bookmarks and folders are deleted with the membership. This cannot be undone.
3. Choose **Continue**.
4. Read the consequence, then choose **Remove member**.

**Result:** SlugBase says the person "was removed from the workspace". They lose access right away, and their API tokens for the workspace stop working. Their tags and remembered choices for slugs are deleted either way, because they are private. Shares they received end. Shares they granted move with transferred content, and go with deleted content.

Removing another owner asks you to confirm it is you.

## Transfer ownership

Transfer ownership when another member should take over the workspace. Only an owner can.

1. Open **Actions for** and the name of a member who is not an owner, and choose **Transfer ownership**.
2. Read **Transfer ownership to** and the person's name. The person becomes an owner. "Owners can also make other members owners."
3. Optional: turn on **Stay as admin** to step down from owner to admin once they are an owner. Leave it off to stay an owner.
4. Type the person's name where the dialog says "Type" and the name "to confirm".
5. Choose **Transfer ownership**.
6. Confirm it is you.

**Result:** SlugBase says the person "is now an owner of the workspace", and with **Stay as admin**, that you are an admin. Where your SlugBase can send email, the new owner gets a message about it.

## Confirm it is you

Some actions here ask for your password again, even though you are signed in: making someone an owner, changing an owner's role, removing an owner, and transferring ownership. SlugBase shows **Confirm it is you**.

1. Enter your **Password**.
2. Choose **Confirm**.

**Result:** the action goes ahead. SlugBase remembers the confirmation for ten minutes. If you close the prompt, nothing changes, and a transfer says "Confirm it is you to transfer ownership." If your account signs in through a provider and has no password, you confirm through the provider instead. If two-factor authentication is on for your account, the prompt also offers **Use a code instead**, which takes the six-digit **Authentication code** from your authenticator app. See [Security](../account/security.mdx).

## Field and option reference

| Field / option | Description | Required |
| -------------- | ----------- | -------- |
| Email addresses | One or more addresses to invite. | Yes |
| Role | **Admin** manages members, invitations and the workspace settings. **Member** uses the workspace's bookmarks and folders. | Yes |
| Teams | Teams the invited people join. | No |
| Transfer to | The member who receives the content of someone who leaves. | Yes, when you transfer |
| Stay as admin | Step down from owner to admin after a transfer. | No |

## Troubleshooting

### The page says "No pending invitations"

Nobody is waiting. People you invite appear here until they join.

### Resend is missing

Your SlugBase cannot send email. Choose **Copy invitation link** and send the link yourself. The page says: "This server cannot send email. Copy the invitation link instead."

### The invitation says "This invitation is no longer pending"

The person joined or someone revoked it. The list reloads.

### A removal says "Nothing was changed"

SlugBase gives the reason. The person may be the last owner, or the member you chose to receive the content may have left the workspace. Choose another member and try again.

### The page says "The members could not be loaded"

Choose **Try again**.

### Former members' content says it could not be loaded

The page says "The bookmarks of former members could not be loaded" or "The folders of former members could not be loaded". Choose **Try again** beside the list that failed. The other list stays as it is.

### Delete selected is off

Nothing is selected. Select at least one row first.

### Reassign to… is off

Nothing is selected, or you selected more than 1,000 bookmarks or more than 1,000 folders. Select at least one row, and reassign larger selections in smaller ones, with each folder selected together with its bookmarks.

### Reassigning former members' content says "The change was not saved"

SlugBase did not move what you selected, and your selection stays. Choose **Reassign to…** again.

### Reassigning says the member is no longer a member of the workspace

The member you chose has left the workspace, or has been removed. Choose another member in **New owner**. Your selection is kept.

### Deleting former members' content says "The change was not saved"

SlugBase did not delete what you selected, or deleted only part of it. When it deleted part, it says how many were deleted before the error and keeps the rest selected. Choose **Delete selected** again.

### Former members' content is empty

Nobody has left content behind. Content appears here when an account is deleted, and when the person chosen to receive a removed member's content had already left the workspace.

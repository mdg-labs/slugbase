## What this is for

The **Profile** page is where you manage your own account: **Email** and **Name**, **Preferences** (language and theme, and **AI suggestions** when that feature is available for your workspace), a shortcut to **Go preferences**, optional [two-factor authentication](/selfhosted/two-factor-authentication), and—on the same screen—personal API tokens and account deletion (those topics have dedicated guides).

## Before you start

- You must be signed in.
- Open **Profile** from the user menu (avatar) in the top bar—the item is labeled **Profile**.

## Open Profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the user menu" icon="user" titleType="p">
    In the top bar, select your avatar or account control to open the menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Profile" icon="user-cog" titleType="p">
    Select **Profile**. The page title is **Profile**, with the subtitle **Manage your account settings and preferences**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The in-app route is **`/profile`** (after your SlugBase app’s base path, if you use one).

## Account

This block is titled **Account**, with the description **Your account details and quick access.**

| Row | What you can do |
| --- | --- |
| **Email** | Select **Edit**, change the address, then **Save** or **Cancel**. If you use OIDC sign-in, the helper text explains that **Email is managed by your identity provider and cannot be changed here.** A pending email change may show **Pending email verification** with instructions. |
| **Name** | Select **Edit**, update your name, then **Save** or **Cancel**. |
| **Quick Access** | Text explains using **`/go/<slug>`** for quick access. Follow **Manage remembered slug choices** to open [Go preferences](/selfhosted/go-preferences). |

## Preferences

This block is titled **Preferences**, with the description **Language, theme, and display options.**

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set language" icon="languages" titleType="p">
    Under **Language**, pick **English** or **Deutsch** (these are the options on the Profile screen today).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set theme" icon="palette" titleType="p">
    Under **Theme**, choose **Auto**, **Light**, or **Dark**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save" icon="save" titleType="p">
    If anything changed, select **Save** at the bottom of the card. While edits are unsaved, you may see **Unsaved changes** next to the button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### AI suggestions

When your workspace exposes this option (the server reports that AI suggestions are available), **Preferences** also includes **AI suggestions** with the description **Suggest title, tags, and slug when creating bookmarks**. Use the toggle to turn that on or off, then **Save** if the footer shows unsaved changes.

<Callout kind="info">
  Workspace-wide AI is configured by an admin. If the toggle does not appear, AI suggestions are not available in your deployment or for your account.
</Callout>

## Admin badge

If you can access workspace administration, an **Admin** badge may appear next to **Signed in as:** and your email on the Profile header.

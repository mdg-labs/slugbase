## What you'll accomplish

After this guide, you can grant read access to bookmarks and folders for individual members or teams in your workspace.

## Prerequisites

- Signed in to the active workspace
- On self-hosted instances, sharing is always available — no plan upgrade required

## How sharing works

Sharing grants **read access** to a bookmark or folder within the same workspace. Shared bookmarks appear in the recipient's lists with a **Shared with you** indicator. Sharing a folder also grants read access to the bookmarks inside it.

Sharing does not copy content — it exposes your existing bookmark or folder to selected members or teams.

## Share a bookmark

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the bookmark menu">
    From the bookmarks list or dashboard, open the menu on the bookmark you own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Share">
    Select **Share** to open the share access dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a grant">
    Choose **Member** or **Team**, select the target, and click **Add**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Close the dialog">
    Click **Done**. Recipients can now find the bookmark under sharing filters.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Share a folder

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the folder">
    From **Folders**, open the menu on a folder you own.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Share">
    Use the same share access dialog as bookmarks. Grants apply to the folder and its bookmarks.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Filter by sharing scope

On bookmark and folder lists, use the **Sharing scope** filter:

| Scope | Shows |
|-------|-------|
| **All bookmarks** / **All folders** | Everything you can access |
| **Only me** | Items you own that are not shared |
| **Shared with me** | Items others shared with you |
| **Shared by me** | Items you shared with others |

## Revoke access

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Share on the item">
    Return to the share access dialog for the bookmark or folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove a grant">
    Next to a member or team, click **Remove**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Bulk share bookmarks

From the bookmarks list, select multiple bookmarks and use the bulk **Share** action to grant access to the same member or team for all selected items.

## Verify it worked

Switch the list filter to **Shared by me** and confirm the item appears. Ask the recipient to check **Shared with me**.

## Troubleshooting

### Share option is missing

Only the owner of a bookmark or folder can manage sharing. You cannot share items owned by another member unless they share with you first (read-only).

### Recipient cannot see the bookmark

Confirm they are a member of the active workspace and that you shared with the correct member or team.

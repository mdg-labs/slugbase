## What you'll learn

After reading this guide, you will understand what a workspace is, how your active workspace affects bookmarks and slugs, and how self-hosted instances use workspaces to separate teams or departments.

## What is a workspace?

A **workspace** is the top-level container for everything you work with in SlugBase: bookmarks, folders, tags, teams, slugs, and settings. Every piece of data belongs to exactly one workspace.

On a self-hosted instance, an **instance administrator** creates workspaces and invites members into them. Self-hosted is designed as an admin-curated, multi-workspace product — new users are placed into existing workspaces by invitation rather than each receiving their own workspace automatically.

## Active workspace

SlugBase always operates in one **active workspace** at a time. Your bookmarks list, slug forwarding, search results, and settings apply to the active workspace.

Your active workspace is stored in your signed-in session. Switching workspaces is an explicit action — SlugBase never mixes data across workspaces.

## Switch workspaces

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the workspace switcher">
    In the sidebar, click **Workspaces**, or press `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` and choose **Switch workspace**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a workspace">
    Pick the workspace you want to work in. SlugBase reloads your session context for that workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You only see workspaces where you are a member. If you lose access to your active workspace, SlugBase automatically moves you to another workspace you still belong to.

## Create a workspace (instance administrators)

Instance administrators can create additional workspaces from the workspace switcher (**New workspace**). On self-hosted instances, workspace creation is unrestricted — there are no plan-based limits.

After creating a workspace, invite members from **Settings → Administration → Members & teams**.

## Personal vs. team workspaces

There is no separate "personal" or "team" workspace type. A workspace with one member behaves like a personal space; a workspace with multiple members and teams is collaborative. The product experience is the same — only membership differs.

## Roles within a workspace

Each member has one of three roles:

| Role | Capabilities |
|------|----------------|
| **Owner** | Full control, including workspace deletion and ownership transfer. At least one owner is required. |
| **Admin** | Manage members, teams, workspace settings, and administration features. |
| **Member** | Create and organize bookmarks, use slugs and forwarding, and access content shared with them. |

Roles are per workspace. You can be an owner in one workspace and a member in another.

## Leaving a workspace

When you leave (or are removed from) a workspace, your **membership** is revoked. Bookmarks, folders, and tags you created **remain in the workspace**. There is no automatic transfer of your content to another workspace.

If you need a copy of your bookmarks before leaving, export them first. See [Backup and export](/selfhosted/backup-and-export).

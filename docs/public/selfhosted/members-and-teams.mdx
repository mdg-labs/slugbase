## What you'll accomplish

After this guide, you can invite members to a workspace, assign roles, and create teams for sharing bookmarks and folders.

## Prerequisites

- Signed in as a workspace **owner** or **admin**
- On self-hosted instances, member management is always available — there is no plan gate

## Open member settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    From the sidebar, select **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Members & teams">
    Under **Administration**, choose **Members & teams**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Invite a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Invite member">
    Enter the person's email address and choose a role: **Member**, **Admin**, or **Owner**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send the invite">
    Click **Send invite**. The recipient receives an email with a link to join the workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Pending invitations appear in the **Pending invitations** section. You can **Resend** or **Revoke** an invitation before it is accepted.

<Callout kind="tip">
Configure [SMTP](/selfhosted/smtp) before sending invitations. Without outbound email, invitation links cannot be delivered.
</Callout>

## Member roles

| Role | Typical use |
|------|-------------|
| **Owner** | Workspace creator or billing owner. Can delete the workspace and transfer ownership. |
| **Admin** | Day-to-day administration — members, teams, workspace settings, audit log. |
| **Member** | Uses bookmarks, slugs, and shared content without admin access. |

To change a member's role, use the role dropdown next to their name.

## Transfer ownership

Only an owner can transfer workspace ownership:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the role menu">
    Next to the target member, open the role dropdown and choose **Transfer ownership…**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Confirm the transfer. You become an admin; the selected member becomes the owner. This cannot be undone.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Remove a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Remove">
    Next to the member, choose **Remove**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm removal">
    Confirm the action. The member loses workspace access. Their bookmarks, folders, and tags remain in the workspace.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Create and manage teams

Teams group members for sharing. Bookmarks and folders can be shared with a team instead of individuals.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Teams tab">
    In **Members & teams**, switch to **Teams**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a team">
    Click **New team**, enter a name and optional description, then **Create team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add members">
    Expand the team and use **Add member** to include workspace members.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Deleting a team removes the grouping only — members keep their workspace access, and bookmarks are not deleted.

## Verify it worked

The new member appears in the **Members** list after accepting an invitation. Teams appear on the **Teams** tab with their member counts.

## Troubleshooting

### Invitation email never arrives

Check [SMTP settings](/selfhosted/smtp) and send a test message. Verify the recipient's spam folder.

### I don't see Members & teams

You need owner or admin role in the active workspace. Members without admin rights cannot open this page.

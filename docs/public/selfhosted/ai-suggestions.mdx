## What you'll accomplish

After this guide, you can enable AI suggestions for your workspace, supply an API credential, and understand how members can opt out individually.

## What AI suggestions do

When enabled, SlugBase can suggest bookmark **titles**, **slugs**, and **tags** based on a destination URL (and fetched page metadata). Suggestions appear when you create or edit a bookmark.

AI is optional. Until you configure a credential and enable suggestions, the feature remains off.

## Prerequisites

- Signed in as a workspace **owner** or **admin**
- An API key from your AI provider (OpenAI in v1)
- Outbound HTTPS from your instance to the provider

## Configure AI suggestions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open AI suggestions">
    Go to **Settings → Workspace → AI suggestions** (labeled **AI** in the section navigation).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable suggestions">
    Turn on **Enable AI suggestions**. This applies workspace-wide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter API key">
    Paste your **API key** in the credential field. The key is stored encrypted and is never logged or exposed after save. Leave the field blank on later edits to keep the current key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a model">
    Select a **Model** (for example GPT-4o mini for faster responses or GPT-4o for higher quality).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Member opt-out

Individual members can disable AI on their own content from **Settings → Account → Preferences → AI suggestions opt-out**, even when the workspace has AI enabled.

## Operator-supplied credentials

Some deployments configure AI credentials at the operator level. In that case, the workspace form may show a notice that AI uses the operator-configured credential, and members can still opt out in preferences.

## Verify it worked

Create or edit a bookmark with a public URL. Suggestion fields should populate after a short delay when AI is available.

## Troubleshooting

| Symptom | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| No suggestions appear | Confirm AI is enabled, a valid API key is saved, and outbound HTTPS is allowed |
| Suggestions in wrong language | SlugBase uses the member's language preference for output |
| Member sees no AI | Check whether they enabled **AI suggestions opt-out** in account preferences |

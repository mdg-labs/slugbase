## What you'll accomplish

After this guide, you can update your profile, secure your account with MFA, and set personal preferences that apply across workspaces.

## Open account settings

From the sidebar, select **Settings**. Under **Account**, choose a section: **Profile**, **Password**, **Two-factor auth**, **API tokens**, or **Preferences**.

## Profile

Update your **Display name** and **Email address**. Your name and email are visible to other members in workspaces you belong to.

Changing your email sends a verification link to the new address. Your email does not change until you confirm the link. You can **Resend verification email** or **Cancel change** while verification is pending.

## Password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Password">
    Under **Account**, select **Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter credentials">
  If you already have a password, enter your **Current password**, then your **New password** and confirmation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Update password**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you signed in with a federated identity provider (OIDC), you may not have a password yet. You can **Add password** to enable direct email-and-password sign-in alongside SSO.

## Two-factor authentication

Protect your account with a time-based authenticator app (TOTP).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Two-factor auth">
    Under **Account**, select **Two-factor auth**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set up authenticator app">
    Click **Set up authenticator app**. Scan the QR code or enter the setup key manually in your authenticator app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify and activate">
    Enter the 6-digit code from your app and click **Verify and activate**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save backup codes">
    Copy your one-time backup codes and store them securely. Each code works only once if you lose your authenticator.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After enrollment, password sign-in requires your authenticator code. Federated (OIDC) sign-in and [API tokens](/selfhosted/api-tokens) do not prompt for MFA.

To disable MFA, enter your current authenticator code on the same page.

<Callout kind="tip">
If you are locked out, an [instance administrator](/selfhosted/instance-admin) can reset MFA on your account.
</Callout>

## Preferences

Personal preferences apply to your account only — not the whole workspace:

| Setting | Options |
|---------|---------|
| **Language** | English, Deutsch (German) |
| **Theme** | Light, Dark, Auto |
| **Accent color** | Periwinkle, Indigo, Teal, and others |
| **Default bookmark view** | Card grid or Table |
| **AI suggestions opt-out** | Disable AI features on your content when the workspace has AI enabled |

Click **Save changes** after editing preferences.

## API tokens

Personal API tokens authenticate scripts and integrations as you. See the dedicated [API tokens](/selfhosted/api-tokens) guide for creation and revocation steps.

## Verify it worked

- Profile changes appear in the account menu and to workspace members.
- After enabling MFA, your next password sign-in prompts for a code.
- Preference changes take effect immediately in the UI.

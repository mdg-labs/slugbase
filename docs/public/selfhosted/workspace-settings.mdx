## What you'll accomplish

After this guide, you can update general workspace settings and understand which configuration sections are available to workspace admins on self-hosted instances.

## Prerequisites

- Signed in as a workspace **owner** or **admin**
- Active workspace selected

## Open workspace settings

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Settings">
    From the sidebar, select **Settings**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Workspace">
    Under **Workspace**, choose **General** or another section from the workspace settings navigation.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Members without admin rights see a notice that workspace settings are restricted to admins and owners.

## General settings

On the **General** section, update the **Workspace name** and click **Save changes**. The name appears in the workspace switcher and member-facing screens.

## Workspace settings sections (self-hosted)

On a typical self-hosted instance with admin UI enabled, workspace admins see these sections under **Workspace**:

| Section | Purpose |
|---------|---------|
| **General** | Workspace name and deletion |
| **Email / SMTP** | Outbound email transport — see [SMTP](/selfhosted/smtp) |
| **AI suggestions** | Enable AI and configure credentials — see [AI suggestions](/selfhosted/ai-suggestions) |
| **Identity providers** | OIDC federated sign-in — see [OIDC](/selfhosted/oidc) |

If your operator manages email or identity centrally, the **Email / SMTP** or **Identity providers** sections may show a read-only notice instead of editable fields.

The **Billing** group does not appear on self-hosted instances.

## Delete a workspace

<Callout kind="danger">
Deleting a workspace permanently removes all bookmarks, folders, tags, teams, and settings in that workspace. This cannot be undone.
</Callout>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open General settings">
    Scroll to **Delete workspace**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start deletion">
    Click **Delete workspace…** and type the workspace name to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm permanently">
    Click **Delete permanently**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Only workspace owners can delete a workspace. At least one owner must remain until deletion is confirmed.

## Verify it worked

After saving general settings, the updated workspace name appears in the sidebar switcher. Configuration changes in SMTP, AI, or OIDC sections show success toasts when saved.

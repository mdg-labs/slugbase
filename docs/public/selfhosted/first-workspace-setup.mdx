## What you'll accomplish

After this guide, your self-hosted instance has an administrator account, a first **workspace**, and you are ready to save bookmarks or invite members.

## Prerequisites

- Completed [Quick start](/selfhosted/quick-start) — the container is running and healthy
- Browser access to your instance URL
- Setup has not been completed yet (you see **Set up your instance** at `/setup`)

## How setup fits self-hosted

Self-hosted SlugBase is an **admin-curated, multi-workspace** product. The account you create during setup becomes the **instance-wide administrator**. That administrator creates workspaces and invites additional members — public registration is off by default (`PUBLIC_REGISTRATION=false`).

Hosted SlugBase auto-creates a personal workspace at signup. On self-hosted, the first workspace is created in this setup wizard instead.

## Step 1 — Open the setup wizard

Navigate to your instance URL. If setup has not run, you are sent to `/setup` automatically.

The page title is **Set up your instance**. The subtitle explains that you will create the initial admin account and your first workspace.

## Step 2 — Create the admin account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Fill in **Your name**, **Email**, and **Password**. Passwords must meet the minimum length shown on the form.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the security note">
    The account you create here is the **instance administrator**. You can add more users and workspaces after setup.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
Store administrator credentials in your team password manager. Recovery without email requires operator intervention if SMTP is not configured yet.
</Callout>

## Step 3 — Create the first workspace

Below the admin section, complete **First workspace**:

| Field | Guidance |
|-------|----------|
| **Workspace name** | Display name members see (for example your company or team name) |
| **Workspace slug** | Lowercase letters, digits, and hyphens only — used internally |

Click **Finish setup**. The button shows **Setting up…** while the instance initializes.

## Step 4 — Complete the getting started guide

After setup, SlugBase walks you through a short onboarding flow:

1. **Welcome to SlugBase** — confirms your workspace is ready
2. **Import your bookmarks** — optional browser export (Netscape HTML or JSON from Chrome, Firefox, Safari)
3. **Set up the browser shortcut** — add a custom search engine so you can type a keyword plus slug in the address bar
4. **You're ready** — summary before **Go to dashboard**

You can skip import and the browser shortcut and finish them later from **Home** or **Settings**.

## Step 5 — Explore Home

The dashboard (**Home**) shows:

- **Getting started** checklist — import, folder, tag, and browser shortcut tasks
- **Pinned** and **Recently used** bookmarks
- **Quick access** — most-used slugs
- Workspace stats for bookmarks, folders, and tags

Use the sidebar to open **Bookmarks**, **Folders**, **Tags**, and **Go / Slugs**.

## Verify it worked

- You can sign out and sign in again with the administrator email
- **Home** shows your workspace name in the subtitle
- **Workspaces** in the top bar lists your first workspace
- Creating a test bookmark from **New bookmark** succeeds

## Invite additional members

After setup, add users by invitation rather than open registration:

1. Sign in as the instance administrator
2. Open **Settings → Members** (when member management is enabled for your plan entitlements)
3. Invite members into the appropriate workspace

If you need self-service signup, an operator can enable public registration — see operator documentation for `PUBLIC_REGISTRATION`.

## Troubleshooting

### "This instance is already configured"

Setup runs once. Sign in with your administrator account or contact whoever completed setup.

### Import failed during onboarding

Confirm the file is a supported browser export (Netscape `.html` or JSON). Large imports may take a moment — wait for the success message showing how many bookmarks were imported.

### I closed onboarding early

Open **Home** and use the **Getting started** checklist to import bookmarks or set up the browser shortcut later.

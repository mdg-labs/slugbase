## What you'll accomplish

After this guide, you have a SlugBase Cloud account with a verified email address and can sign in to your workspace.

## Before you start

- A valid email address you can access immediately
- A password of at least 8 characters
- A modern web browser with cookies enabled

## Create your account

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open SlugBase Cloud">
    Go to [cloud.slugbase.app](https://cloud.slugbase.app) and choose **Create account** (or open the sign-up page directly from the sign-in screen).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    Fill in your full name, email address, and password. The password must be at least 8 characters.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit registration">
    Click **Create account**. SlugBase creates your user account and provisions your first workspace on the Free plan.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verify your email

SlugBase Cloud requires email verification before you can use the app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your inbox">
    After registration, SlugBase sends a verification link to the email address you provided. Open the link in the same browser when possible.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm verification">
    When verification succeeds, you see a confirmation screen. You can now sign in and access your workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resend if needed">
    If the link expired or you did not receive the email, use **Resend verification email** on the verify screen. Wait a few minutes between attempts if you hit a rate limit.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Callout kind="tip">
If you made a typo in your email address, use **Update and resend** on the verify screen to correct it and receive a new link.
</Callout>

## Sign in

<Steps>
  <Step title="Return to sign in">
    Go to [cloud.slugbase.app](https://cloud.slugbase.app) and choose **Sign in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter credentials">
    Type your email and password. Optionally enable **Keep me signed in on this device** for a longer session on trusted devices.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use SSO if available">
    If your organization uses single sign-on, choose **Sign in with** your identity provider instead of a password.
  </Step>
</Steps>

After a successful sign-in, SlugBase opens the getting-started onboarding flow for your new workspace. Continue with [First workspace](/cloud/first-workspace).

## Verify it worked

You reach the SlugBase app shell with your workspace name visible in the workspace switcher. The dashboard shows your workspace overview and a getting-started checklist.

## Troubleshooting

### I did not receive the verification email

Check spam or promotions folders. Confirm the email address is correct using **Update and resend** on the verify screen. Wait a few minutes before requesting another email.

### "An account with that email already exists"

An account is already registered with that address. Use **Sign in** or **Forgot password?** instead of creating a new account.

### "Incorrect email or password" after verification

Confirm caps lock is off and try **Forgot password?** to reset your password. SlugBase does not reveal whether an email is registered on the reset flow — if an account exists, instructions are sent.

### Registration is not available

Public registration may be disabled on some deployments. SlugBase Cloud enables registration by default; if you see this message, contact support.

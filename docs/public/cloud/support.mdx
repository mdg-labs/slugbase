## Workspace-level help

For questions about shared content, member access, or workspace policy, contact your workspace **owner** or **admin**:

- Invitations and role changes — **Settings → Members & teams** (Team plan)
- Sharing and teams — workspace admin
- Workspace name and settings — workspace admin or owner

SlugBase support cannot override workspace admin decisions or grant access to a workspace without owner approval.

## Contact SlugBase support

Email **[support@slugbase.app](mailto:support@slugbase.app)** for:

- Account access recovery (lost MFA and backup codes)
- Billing and subscription issues
- Bugs or unexpected behaviour on SlugBase Cloud
- Data and privacy questions

<Callout kind="tip">
Include your account email, the workspace name, and steps to reproduce the issue. Screenshots help, but never send passwords or API tokens.
</Callout>

### Response expectations

Support is provided during business hours (Central European Time). Most requests receive a reply within two business days. Billing emergencies (failed payments blocking access) are prioritised.

## Marketing and sales

For pricing questions, product feedback, or partnership enquiries, use the contact form on the [SlugBase website](https://slugbase.app).

## Security reports

If you discover a security vulnerability, report it responsibly to **support@slugbase.app** with "Security" in the subject line. Do not disclose issues publicly before we have had a chance to respond.

<Callout kind="danger">
Never share your password, MFA backup codes, or API tokens in support email.
</Callout>

## Status and incidents

For platform-wide outages or degraded performance, check the status page linked from the SlugBase website or follow announcements from MDG Labs.

## Contact the team

Use the **Contact** page on the marketing site: [slugbase.app/contact](https://slugbase.app/contact). The form asks for **Name**, **Email**, and **Message**; after you send, the page confirms success or shows an error. A short **privacy notice** on submit explains that your data is processed per the Privacy Policy.

The Contact page copy notes that questions about self-hosting, teams, or billing usually get a reply within **a few business days**.

## Documentation and source code

- **Documentation** (guides for self-hosted and Cloud): [docs.slugbase.app](https://docs.slugbase.app)
- **Open-source repository**: [github.com/mdg-labs/slugbase](https://github.com/mdg-labs/slugbase)

## Support expectations by plan

Marketing copy for **Free** lists **community support**; **Personal** and higher tiers describe **priority support** and backups where applicable (see [Plans and limits](/cloud/plans-and-limits) and [pricing](https://slugbase.app/pricing) for the latest positioning).

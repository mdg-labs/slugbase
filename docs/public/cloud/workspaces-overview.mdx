## What is a workspace?

A **workspace** is the top-level container for your SlugBase data. Everything you save — bookmarks, folders, tags, slugs, and sharing — belongs to exactly one workspace at a time.

When you sign up on SlugBase Cloud, you get a personal workspace automatically. You can belong to additional workspaces when another member invites you.

## Active workspace

SlugBase always works in one **active workspace** at a time. Bookmarks, search, forwarding, and settings apply to that workspace until you switch.

## Creating workspaces

How many workspaces you can **own** depends on your plan:

| Plan | Workspaces you can own |
|------|------------------------|
| Free | 1 |
| Personal | Multiple |
| Team | Multiple |

If you are on the Free plan and try to create a second workspace, SlugBase offers an upgrade to Personal or Team.

You can still **join** workspaces owned by others through invitations, regardless of your own plan.

## Roles in a workspace

Every member has one of three roles:

| Role | Typical permissions |
|------|---------------------|
| **Owner** | Full control, including billing and workspace deletion. Each workspace must have at least one owner. |
| **Admin** | Manage members, teams, and workspace settings. Billing changes may be limited to the owner. |
| **Member** | Create and organize bookmarks, use slugs and forwarding, and access shared content. |

Roles are per workspace. You might be an owner of your personal workspace and a member of a team workspace at the same time.

## Personal vs team workspaces

There is no separate "personal workspace" or "team workspace" entity. A workspace becomes collaborative when it has multiple members and the right plan features.

- **Personal** and **Free** plans are designed for individual use — team administration and sharing features are not available.
- The **Team** plan unlocks member invitations, teams, bookmark and folder sharing, and the audit log.

See [Members and teams](/cloud/members-and-teams) for collaboration features and [Plans and billing](/cloud/plans-and-billing) for plan details.

## Leaving a workspace

When you leave a workspace (or an admin removes you), your **membership** ends. Bookmarks, folders, and tags you created **stay in the workspace** — they are not moved to another workspace.

If you want to keep your content, [export your bookmarks](/cloud/search-and-import) before leaving.

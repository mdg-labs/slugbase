## Plans and pricing

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Is SlugBase Cloud free?" default-open="false">
    Yes. The **Free** plan includes core bookmark features with **up to 50 bookmarks**—no credit card required. Paid plans add unlimited bookmarks, **AI** title/tag/slug suggestions, backups, and **team** features on **Team**. See [Plans and limits](/cloud/plans-and-limits).
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What do Personal and Team cost?" default-open="false">
    Product copy lists **Personal** at **€3/mo** or **€30/year** and **Team** at **€9/mo** or **€90/yr** with **5 users included** and per-user add-ons. **VAT** may be added at checkout depending on location. Confirm current numbers on [Pricing](https://slugbase.app/pricing).
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What is Early Supporter?" default-open="false">
    **Early Supporter** is a **€69 one-time** tier described as lifetime **Personal**-class benefits with **limited seats** and launch-window messaging. It is **not** a Team plan. In billing APIs the plan id is `supporter`. See [Plans and limits](/cloud/plans-and-limits).
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Features and limits

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="When do I get AI suggestions?" default-open="false">
    **AI** suggestions are off on **Free**. **Personal**, **Team**, and **Early Supporter** include them once your plan has loaded in the app. See [Plans and limits](/cloud/plans-and-limits).
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="When can my team share bookmarks and folders?" default-open="false">
    **Team sharing** UI (share actions, scope tabs, **Members** / **Teams** admin on Cloud) requires the **Team** plan. Other plans keep libraries non-collaborative in that sense. Details: [Collaboration](/cloud/collaboration).
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Do I need a browser extension?" default-open="false">
    No. SlugBase runs as a **web app**; slugs work through your normal browsing flow. Optional browser setup is documented in [Browser setup](/selfhosted/browser-setup).
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Account and billing

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="Why do I need to verify my email?" default-open="false">
    Cloud enables **required email verification**. After sign-up you follow the link in email before logging in. Resend and typo fixes are described in [Sign up and login](/cloud/sign-up-and-login).
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="How do I update payment method or cancel?" default-open="false">
    In **Admin → Billing**, use **Manage subscription** to open the **Stripe Customer Portal**. Cancellation is billed through the end of the period per in-app copy. See [Billing](/cloud/billing).
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="What is a workspace?" default-open="false">
    Cloud scopes data and billing to a **workspace**. New users get a default workspace automatically. See [Workspaces](/cloud/workspaces).
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

## Self-hosted vs Cloud

<ExpandableGroup>
  <Expandable title="What is the difference between SlugBase Cloud and self-hosted?" default-open="false">
    **Cloud** is the managed service at **slugbase.app** with workspaces and Stripe billing. **Self-hosted** is the open-source core you run yourself. Feature parity for the app itself is documented in [Self-hosted guides](/selfhosted/introduction); Cloud-only topics are under [Cloud introduction](/cloud/introduction).
  </Expandable>

  <Expandable title="Where is the open-source code?" default-open="false">
    [github.com/mdg-labs/slugbase](https://github.com/mdg-labs/slugbase)
  </Expandable>
</ExpandableGroup>

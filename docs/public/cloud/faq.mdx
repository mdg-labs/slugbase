## General

### What is SlugBase Cloud?

SlugBase Cloud is the hosted version of SlugBase — a bookmark manager with built-in URL shortening and keyboard-driven navigation. Save bookmarks, assign short **slugs**, and jump to any URL from the address bar or command palette with `/go/<slug>`.

See [Introduction](/cloud/introduction) for an overview.

### How is Cloud different from self-hosted?

SlugBase Cloud is operated by MDG Labs with managed infrastructure, Stripe billing, and public sign-up. Self-hosted runs on your own infrastructure with operator-controlled configuration and no cloud billing screens.

The same application powers both — features differ by **plan**, not by a separate product fork.

## Accounts and sign-up

### How do I create an account?

Visit [cloud.slugbase.app](https://cloud.slugbase.app) and follow the sign-up flow. Email verification is required before full access.

See [Sign up](/cloud/sign-up).

### Can I use SlugBase without a password?

Yes, if your workspace configures federated sign-in through an identity provider. You can also add a password later from account settings.

### How many workspaces can I own?

| Plan | Owned workspaces |
|------|------------------|
| Free | 1 |
| Personal | Multiple |
| Team | Multiple |

You can **join** other people's workspaces through invitations regardless of your plan.

## Plans and billing

### What plans are available?

- **Free** — 50 bookmarks per workspace, one owned workspace
- **Personal** — unlimited bookmarks, AI suggestions, multiple owned workspaces
- **Team** — collaboration features, per-seat pricing (minimum 2 seats)

See [Plans and billing](/cloud/plans-and-billing) for the full comparison.

### Is the paid individual tier called "Pro"?

No. The paid individual tier is **Personal**.

### Are folders limited on Free?

No. Folders are unlimited on every plan.

### Are API tokens limited by plan?

No. API tokens are available on all plans. Each account has a maximum number of active tokens.

### What happens to my bookmarks if I cancel?

When your subscription ends, the workspace returns to Free. If you have more than 50 bookmarks, the excess are **archived** (hidden but preserved). Upgrade again to restore them.

### Where do I find pricing?

Current prices appear in **Settings → Billing** and on the [SlugBase website](https://slugbase.app).

## Workspaces and collaboration

### What is a workspace?

A workspace is the container for bookmarks, folders, tags, slugs, members, and settings. See [Workspaces overview](/cloud/workspaces-overview).

### How do I invite teammates?

Member invitations require the **Team** plan. Open **Settings → Members & teams** and send an invite.

See [Members and teams](/cloud/members-and-teams).

### What are the member roles?

**Owner** (billing and full control), **Admin** (members and settings), and **Member** (use the product). Roles are per workspace.

### Can I take my bookmarks when I leave a workspace?

Your bookmarks stay in the workspace when you leave. Export them first if you need a personal copy.

## Slugs and forwarding

### Are slugs public short links?

No. Slugs resolve only for **signed-in workspace members**. Anonymous `/go` resolution is not available.

### Can two people use the same slug?

Yes, if they own different bookmarks. Slugs are unique per owner within a workspace.

See [Slugs and go](/cloud/slugs-and-go).

## Security

### Should I enable MFA?

Yes. Two-factor authentication significantly improves account security. See [MFA and security](/cloud/mfa-and-security).

### Do API tokens bypass MFA?

Yes. API tokens are long-lived credentials you create deliberately. Protect them like passwords.

### Does SlugBase support SSO?

Workspaces can configure OIDC identity providers for federated sign-in. Availability depends on workspace settings.

## Data and privacy

### Can I export my bookmarks?

Yes. Export produces a lossless JSON file with bookmarks, folders, tags, and slugs. See [Search and import](/cloud/search-and-import).

### Where is my data stored?

SlugBase Cloud runs in the EU (Frankfurt region). See the privacy policy on the marketing site for details.

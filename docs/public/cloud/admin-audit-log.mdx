## When you see Audit log

On **SlugBase Cloud**, **Admin → Audit log** is available only on the **Team** plan. The product description in the app also states that on SlugBase Cloud the audit log is available on the Team plan.

Until your workspace’s plan has finished loading in the browser, the **Audit log** entry may be hidden. If your plan does not include the audit log, you are redirected to another Admin section you can use (for example **Members** when that tab is available).

## How to open it

In the sidebar under **Admin**, choose **Audit log**. (Direct URL: `https://slugbase.app/app/admin/audit-log`)

## What it is for

Use **Audit log** to review recent changes affecting bookmarks, folders, tags, teams, and members—see the self-hosted guide for column meanings and loading more events.

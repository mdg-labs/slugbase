## Overview

A **slug** is a short keyword on a bookmark. When **forwarding** is enabled, signed-in workspace members can open `/go/<slug>` to jump to the bookmark's destination URL.

Slugs are SlugBase's signature feature — they power address-bar shortcuts, the command palette's `go` mode, and the dashboard quick-access section.

## How forwarding works

<Steps>
  <Step title="Assign a slug">
    When creating or editing a bookmark, enter a slug and enable **Enable forwarding**. A slug is required when forwarding is on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the forwarding address">
    The modal shows your forwarding address (for example, `/go/docs`). Slugs are unique per owner within a workspace — two members can each use `docs` on their own bookmarks.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the destination">
    Visit `/go/<slug>` while signed in, type `go <slug>` in the command palette, or use your browser search shortcut.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<!-- screenshot:go-forwarding-settings -->

<Callout kind="info">
Slugs are **private to your workspace**. Forwarding requires an authenticated session — there are no public short links or anonymous slug resolution in v1.
</Callout>

## The /go redirect flow

When you open `/go/<slug>`:

1. **Not signed in** — SlugBase sends you to sign in, then returns you to the redirect.
2. **No match** — No accessible bookmark with forwarding enabled matches the slug.
3. **One match** — Immediate forward to the destination; usage stats update in the background.
4. **Multiple matches** — A disambiguation page lists matching bookmarks. Pick one, or choose **Always open with this bookmark** to remember your preference.

## Remembered slug preferences

When a slug matches more than one bookmark you can access, you can save a per-user preference so future visits skip disambiguation.

Manage preferences under **Go / Slugs** in the app (remembered preferences list). From there you can:

- See which slug maps to which bookmark
- **Change preferred bookmark** while the slug is still ambiguous
- **Remove preference** to return to the disambiguation page

## Browser search shortcut

Register SlugBase as a custom search engine in your browser so typing a keyword plus a slug in the address bar forwards instantly.

During first-login onboarding, SlugBase provides the URL template and keyword to copy. You can also revisit setup from the dashboard **Getting started** checklist (**Set up browser search shortcut**).

| Field | What to enter |
|-------|---------------|
| Name | SlugBase (or any label) |
| Keyword | Short prefix such as `go` |
| URL | SlugBase forwarding URL with `%s` where the slug goes |

Test by typing your keyword, a space, and a slug in the address bar.

## Command palette go mode

Press `⌘K` / `Ctrl+K` and type `go ` followed by a slug to forward without leaving the app. See [Command palette](/cloud/command-palette) for full palette usage.

## Verify it worked

Open `/go/<your-slug>` in a signed-in browser tab. You arrive at the bookmark's destination URL. Check **Quick access** on the dashboard — frequently used slugs appear there.

## Common questions

### Can I share a slug with someone outside my workspace?

No. Slugs resolve only for signed-in members of the workspace with access to the bookmark.

### What happens if I disable forwarding?

The slug can remain on the bookmark, but `/go/<slug>` will not forward until forwarding is enabled again.

### Can two members use the same slug?

Yes — slugs are unique per **owner**, not globally per workspace. Disambiguation appears when multiple accessible bookmarks share a slug.

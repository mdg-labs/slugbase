## What you'll accomplish

After this guide, you can save a bookmark, edit it in the modal editor, pin it for quick access, and filter your bookmark list.

## Prerequisites

- Signed in to SlugBase Cloud
- Active workspace selected
- Room under your plan's bookmark limit (Free: **50** bookmarks per workspace)

<!-- screenshot:bookmarks-list-overview -->

## Create a bookmark

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Bookmarks">
    From the sidebar, select **Bookmarks**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start a new bookmark">
    Click **New bookmark** (or press `C` from the bookmarks list when no modal is open).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter URL and title">
  Paste the destination URL and add a readable title. SlugBase may fetch page metadata to help fill the title.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Optional: slug, folders, tags">
    Assign a **slug** and enable **forwarding** to jump to this link via `/go/<slug>`. Add **folders** and **tags** to organize the bookmark. Toggle **Pin bookmark** to surface it on your dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save bookmark**. The bookmark appears in your list immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Edit or delete a bookmark

Bookmark editing happens in a **modal** — there is no separate bookmark detail page.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the bookmark menu">
    From the grid or table view, open the **More options** menu on a bookmark row.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Edit">
    Update the title, URL, slug, forwarding, folders, tags, or pin state. Click **Save changes**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delete if needed">
    Choose **Delete** and confirm. Deletion is permanent — there is no trash in v1.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pin bookmarks

**Pinning** is the only prominence mechanism in SlugBase — there are no favorites or stars.

Pinned bookmarks appear:

- In the **Pinned** section on the bookmarks list
- On your **dashboard** home surface

Open the bookmark menu and choose **Pin** or **Unpin**. You can also pin multiple bookmarks using bulk selection.

## Filter, sort, and search

The bookmarks list supports:

| Control | Options |
|---------|---------|
| **Search** | Match title, URL, or slug |
| **Folder filter** | All folders or a specific folder |
| **Tag filter** | One or more tags |
| **Pinned filter** | Show pinned bookmarks only |
| **Sort** | Recently added, alphabetical, most used, recently accessed |
| **View** | Grid or table — your preference is remembered |

Use **Clear filters** when no results match your current filters.

## Bulk actions

<Steps>
  <Step title="Enter selection mode">
    Click **Select** on the bookmarks list, then choose individual bookmarks or select all across pages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a bulk action">
    Available actions include **Delete**, **Move to folder**, **Pin**, **Unpin**, and **Tags** (add or remove).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Complete the action. Bulk tag changes show a preview before applying.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Free plan bookmark limit

On the **Free** plan, each workspace holds up to **50** bookmarks. When you approach the cap, SlugBase shows a banner with how many bookmarks remain. At the cap, you cannot create or import more bookmarks until you upgrade to **Personal** or remove existing bookmarks.

<Callout kind="tip">
Upgrade to **Personal** for unlimited bookmarks. Open **Settings → Billing** or use the **Upgrade to Personal** action on the banner.
</Callout>

## AI suggestions (Personal and above)

On **Personal** and **Team** plans, the bookmark modal can suggest titles, slugs, and tags based on the destination URL. AI suggestions are unavailable on Free. You can opt out of AI in workspace settings.

## Verify it worked

Your new bookmark appears in the list with the correct title and URL. If you enabled forwarding and assigned a slug, open `/go/<slug>` or use the command palette to test forwarding.

## Troubleshooting

### The URL was rejected

Enter a valid `http://` or `https://` URL. URLs must be 2048 characters or fewer.

### I cannot save more bookmarks

You may have reached the Free plan cap of **50** bookmarks. Upgrade to **Personal** or delete bookmarks you no longer need.

### Slug validation errors

Slugs must start with a letter or number and use lowercase letters, digits, and hyphens only. Some slugs are reserved by the system.

### Pin is not available

You need permission to edit the bookmark. Shared bookmarks follow the owner's sharing rules.

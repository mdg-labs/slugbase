## How the product enforces plans

After you sign in on Cloud, the app loads your workspace’s **plan** from the server and uses it (once loading finishes) to show or hide team sharing, certain admin tabs, AI surfaces, and bookmark limit messaging. Self-hosted installs do not use this Cloud plan gate.

| Plan | Bookmarks | AI suggestions | Team sharing / collaboration UI |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Free** | **50** max | **No** | **No** |
| **Personal** | **Unlimited** | **Yes** | **No** |
| **Team** | **Unlimited** | **Yes** | **Yes** |
| **Early Supporter** | **Unlimited** | **Yes** | **No** (same collaboration gating as Personal) |

All paid plans (**Personal**, **Team**, **Early Supporter**) include **AI** suggestions. Only **Team** unlocks sharing UI, **Mine / all / shared** scope tabs on bookmarks and folders, **Members** and **Teams** under Admin (on Cloud), and member invitations.

## Prices and marketing copy

Pricing on [slugbase.app/pricing](https://slugbase.app/pricing) and in-app strings use these anchors (see product locale strings under `pricing`):

| Tier | Price hints in product copy |
| --- | --- |
| **Free** | €0; **50 bookmarks**; no credit card |
| **Personal** | **€3/mo** or **€30/year**; unlimited bookmarks; AI-powered title, tag and slug suggestions; backups and priority support called out in marketing |
| **Team** | **€9/mo** or **€90/yr**; **5 users included**; **+€1** per additional user/mo (or **+€10** per additional user/yr on yearly); shared folders, roles and permissions, light audit log |
| **Early Supporter** | **€69 one-time**; lifetime Personal-class benefits; limited launch window and seats in marketing copy |

<Callout kind="tip">
  **VAT:** In-app pricing text notes that **prices exclude VAT** and applicable sales taxes, which may be calculated at checkout depending on location.
</Callout>

The billing screen may label the Early Supporter tier as **Supporter** in the plan row while marketing uses **Early Supporter**—both refer to the same lifetime tier.

## Team seats

Team subscriptions include **5 seats** by default. You can purchase **extra seats**; billing and admin copy describe prorated charges via Stripe and limits when you have more members than seats (see [Billing](/cloud/billing)).

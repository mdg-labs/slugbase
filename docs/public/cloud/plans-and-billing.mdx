## Overview

Each workspace on SlugBase Cloud has a **plan** that controls bookmarks, AI suggestions, collaboration features, and how many workspaces you can own. Plans and prices are shown in **Settings → Billing** and on the [SlugBase marketing site](https://slugbase.app).

Only the workspace **owner** can change plans, manage seats, or open the billing portal.

## Plans at a glance

| Feature | Free | Personal | Team |
|---------|:----:|:--------:|:----:|
| Bookmarks per workspace | 50 | Unlimited | Unlimited |
| Workspaces you can own | 1 | Multiple | Multiple |
| AI suggestions | — | ✓ | ✓ |
| Member invitations | — | — | ✓ |
| Teams and sharing | — | — | ✓ |
| Audit log | — | — | ✓ |
| Pricing | Free | Per workspace | Per seat (min. 2) |

<Callout kind="info">
**Personal** is the paid individual tier — not "Pro". Folders are unlimited on every plan. API tokens are not plan-gated.
</Callout>

### Free

The entry tier. Up to **50 bookmarks** per workspace and **one workspace** you can own. AI suggestions and team features are not included.

### Personal

For individuals who need unlimited bookmarks, AI suggestions, and multiple owned workspaces. Personal is single-user — no member invitations or team sharing.

### Team

For collaboration. Includes everything in Personal plus member invitations, teams, bookmark and folder sharing, the audit log, and **per-seat pricing** with a **minimum of 2 seats**.

### Supporter / lifetime

During launch, SlugBase may offer a one-time **supporter** purchase that grants **Personal features permanently** to a workspace. It is a promotion, not a separate product tier.

## Upgrade or change plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open billing settings">
    Go to **Settings → Billing** as the workspace owner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare plans">
    Review the plan comparison table. Toggle monthly or annual billing if both are offered.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start checkout">
    Click **Upgrade to Personal** or **Upgrade to Team**. For Team, choose your initial seat count (at least 2), then continue to secure checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Return to SlugBase">
    After payment, your workspace plan updates automatically. Refresh if the UI has not updated yet.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Checkout and payment are handled through Stripe. SlugBase does not store your full card number.

## Manage subscription

From **Settings → Billing**:

- **Manage billing** — open the Stripe customer portal to update payment method, view invoices, or download receipts
- **Cancel subscription** — schedule a return to Free at the end of the current billing period
- **Reactivate** — undo a pending cancellation before the period ends

<Callout kind="tip">
Only the workspace owner sees billing actions. Admins and members can view plan status but cannot change it.
</Callout>

## Seats (Team plan)

Team plans bill per seat. Each member uses one seat.

| Action | Where |
|--------|-------|
| View seat usage | **Settings → Billing** or **Settings → Members & teams** |
| Add seats | **Settings → Billing → Seats** |
| Remove seats | Reduce seats only after removing members, and never below 2 |

Seat changes may be prorated for the current billing period.

## Bookmark limits and usage meter

On the Free plan, a usage meter in the sidebar shows how many of your 50 bookmarks are in use. When you approach the cap, SlugBase shows a banner with an upgrade option.

At the cap, **new bookmark creation and import are blocked** until you upgrade or delete bookmarks.

## Downgrading to Free

When a paid subscription ends — by cancellation or expiry — the workspace returns to Free at the **end of the billing period** (after any grace period for failed payments).

If you have more than 50 bookmarks:

- Excess bookmarks are **archived, not deleted**
- Archived bookmarks are hidden from lists, search, slug forwarding, and counts
- Upgrading again **restores** archived bookmarks automatically

New bookmarks remain blocked while you are at or over the Free cap.

## Billing history

**Settings → Billing** includes links to invoices and payment method management in the Stripe portal. Past payments and PDF downloads appear there after your first subscription.

## Verify it worked

- The current plan badge appears in the sidebar and on the billing page
- Team workspaces show seat usage after members join
- Upgrades unlock the corresponding features immediately (or after checkout redirect)

## Troubleshooting

### I can't change the plan

Only the workspace owner can manage billing. Ask the owner to upgrade or transfer ownership.

### Checkout did not update my plan

Wait a minute and refresh. If the plan is still wrong, open **Manage billing** in the portal or contact [support](/cloud/support).

### I need fewer than 2 Team seats

Team requires a minimum of 2 seats. For solo use, choose Personal instead.

## What you'll accomplish

After this guide, you understand how your first SlugBase Cloud workspace is set up, you have completed (or skipped) the onboarding steps, and you are ready to save bookmarks.

## How your workspace is created

When you register on SlugBase Cloud, SlugBase automatically creates a **personal workspace** for you on the **Free** plan. You do not need to run a setup wizard or invite yourself — your account and workspace are ready after email verification.

On the Free plan:

- You can own **one workspace**
- The workspace includes up to **50** bookmarks
- AI suggestions, team sharing, and audit log are not included

Upgrade to **Personal** or **Team** when you need unlimited bookmarks, additional workspaces, or collaboration features. See [Plans and billing](/cloud/plans-and-billing).

## First-login onboarding

After your first sign-in, SlugBase walks you through a four-step getting-started guide. You can complete each step or skip and return later from the dashboard checklist.

### Step 1 — Welcome

SlugBase confirms your workspace is ready and introduces core concepts: bookmarks, slugs, and keyboard-driven navigation. Click **Get started** to continue.

### Step 2 — Import your bookmarks

Optionally import bookmarks from a browser export:

- **Supported formats:** Netscape HTML (`.html`) or JSON export from Chrome, Firefox, or Safari
- **Drop or browse:** Drag a file into the drop zone or click to browse
- **Skip:** Choose **Continue without importing** if you want to start fresh — you can import later from the dashboard or Bookmarks page

Imported bookmarks count toward your plan's bookmark limit. On Free, imports stop when you reach **50** bookmarks.

### Step 3 — Browser search shortcut

Set up a browser custom search engine so you can type a keyword plus a slug in the address bar to forward instantly (for example, `go docs` → `/go/docs`).

The onboarding guide shows the fields to copy:

| Field | Example |
|-------|---------|
| Name | SlugBase |
| Keyword | `go` (or your preference) |
| URL with `%s` | Your SlugBase forwarding URL with `%s` as the slug placeholder |

Copy the values, add the search engine in your browser settings, and test by typing your keyword followed by a slug. Choose **Skip for now** if you prefer to set this up later.

### Step 4 — Ready

SlugBase summarizes what you completed (import, browser shortcut) and sends you to the **dashboard**.

## Explore the dashboard

The dashboard is your home surface after onboarding:

- **Stats** — bookmark, folder, and tag counts for your workspace
- **Pinned** — bookmarks you have pinned for quick access
- **Quick access** — your most-used slugs
- **Getting started checklist** — dismissible reminders for import, browser shortcut, folder, and tag creation
- **Plan prompts** — upgrade banners when you approach or reach the Free bookmark cap

## Switch or create workspaces

Use the **workspace switcher** in the app shell to see your active workspace. On the Free plan, you own one workspace. To create additional workspaces, upgrade to **Personal** or **Team** — the switcher shows an upgrade prompt when you try to create another workspace on Free.

Active workspace selection is stored in your session. Bookmarks, folders, and slugs always belong to the workspace you have selected.

## Verify it worked

Your dashboard loads with your workspace name in the subtitle. You can open **Bookmarks** from the sidebar and create your first bookmark, or follow the checklist items at your own pace.

## What you'll accomplish

After this guide, you understand how workspace membership works on SlugBase Cloud, including invitations, roles, teams, and seat limits on Team plans.

## Team plan required

Member invitations, role management, teams, bookmark sharing, and the audit log are available on the **Team** plan only.

On Free and Personal plans, the **Members & teams** settings section is hidden and invitation endpoints are unavailable. If you need collaboration, upgrade to Team from **Settings → Billing**.

<Callout kind="info">
API tokens are not plan-gated — any signed-in member can create personal API tokens from account settings.
</Callout>

## Prerequisites

- You are a workspace **owner** or **admin**
- The workspace is on the **Team** plan
- Available seats for new members (see [Seat limits](#seat-limits))

## Invite a member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Members & teams">
    Go to **Settings → Members & teams**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send an invitation">
    Click **Invite member**, enter the colleague's email address, choose a role (**Admin** or **Member**), and send the invite.
  </Step>
  <Step title="They accept the invitation">
    The invitee receives an email with a link. When they accept, they join the workspace and **one seat is consumed**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Invitations expire after a set period. You can resend or revoke pending invitations from the same screen.

<Callout kind="tip">
An invitation reserves a seat **when it is accepted**, not when it is sent.
</Callout>

## Member roles

| Role | Can do |
|------|--------|
| **Owner** | Everything, including billing and deleting the workspace. At least one owner is required. |
| **Admin** | Invite and remove members, manage teams, change workspace settings, view the audit log. |
| **Member** | Use bookmarks, slugs, folders, tags, and shared content. Cannot manage members or billing. |

Only an owner can transfer ownership to another member. After transfer, the previous owner becomes an admin.

## Remove a member

From **Settings → Members & teams**, open a member's menu and choose **Remove**.

Removed members lose access immediately. Their bookmarks, folders, and tags remain in the workspace.

## Teams

Teams group members inside a workspace for organization and sharing. They do not replace workspace membership — every person is still a workspace member.

<!-- screenshot:cloud-teams-list -->

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create a team">
    On the **Teams** tab, click **New team**, enter a name and optional description, and save.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add members to the team">
    Expand the team and add existing workspace members.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Share with the team">
    When sharing a bookmark or folder, choose the team as the audience. See sharing behaviour in your bookmark and folder guides.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Deleting a team removes the grouping only. Members keep workspace access and their bookmarks.

## Seat limits

Team plans are priced **per seat**. Each workspace member occupies one seat.

| Rule | Detail |
|------|--------|
| Minimum seats | Team requires at least **2 seats** at purchase and while subscribed |
| Seat usage | Displayed as "X of Y seats used" in member settings |
| Adding seats | Owners can add seats from **Settings → Billing → Seats** |
| Reducing seats | Cannot go below the current member count or the 2-seat minimum |
| At limit | New invitations are blocked until you purchase more seats |

## Audit log

Workspace owners and admins on Team plans can view the **audit log** under **Settings → Audit log**. It records significant actions such as member changes, bookmark edits, and settings updates.

## Verify it worked

- Invited members appear in the member list (or under pending invitations until they accept)
- Seat usage updates after acceptance
- Teams appear on the Teams tab with the members you added

## Troubleshooting

### I don't see Members & teams

Your workspace is not on the Team plan. Upgrade from **Settings → Billing**.

### Seat limit reached

Purchase additional seats before sending more invitations.

### The invitee didn't receive email

Ask them to check spam. Resend the invitation from the pending list. If problems persist, see [Troubleshooting](/cloud/troubleshooting).

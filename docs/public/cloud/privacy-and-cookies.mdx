## Cookie preferences

When you first visit SlugBase Cloud, a cookie banner lets you choose which cookies to allow:

- **Strictly necessary** — always on; required for login and security.
- **Analytics** — optional; enables usage analytics and error reporting to help improve the product.
- **Functional / Marketing** — reserved for future use.

You can change your choices at any time via **Manage preferences**. For full details on how your data is handled, see the [Privacy Policy](https://slugbase.app/privacy).
